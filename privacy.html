Privacy

90s Foodie is a publication, not a platform: it sets no reader accounts, runs no comment accounts of its own, and does not ask readers for names, addresses, or payment details. There is no sign-up on this site and nothing to log in to.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people visit and how the archive is used. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location (from IP address), device, and browser. We use it only to improve the site. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Cookies. Most browsers let you view, block, or delete cookies in their settings. You can use this site with cookies disabled; the archive, the quizzes, and the trivia work the same — only the analytics are affected.

Contact. If you write to us, we read your message and reply from our own mailbox; we do not sell, rent, or share reader correspondence with anyone.
