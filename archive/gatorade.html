Gatorade

The sports drink that owned every 90s sideline, cooler, and commercial break.

Still sold!
MakerPepsiCo
Years1965–today · 90s flavor boom
StatusStill sold
ShelfDrinks

Born in a lab at the University of Florida in 1965, Gatorade spent the 90s becoming something bigger than a drink: it was the uniform of effort. Every sideline cooler, every post-game handshake, every kid who'd just run one lap and felt like a pro reached for that lightning bolt.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The 90s were its flavor explosion — the decade the lineup went from a couple of classics to a rainbow — and the 'Be Like Mike' era welded it to basketball royalty forever. Still sold everywhere; what's archived here is the decade it became a culture.
