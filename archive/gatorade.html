The story

Born in a University of Florida lab in 1965, Gatorade spent the 90s becoming something bigger than a drink: it was the uniform of effort. Every sideline cooler, every post-game handshake, every kid who'd just run one lap and felt like a pro reached for that lightning bolt.

The 90s were its flavor explosion — the decade the lineup went from a couple of classics to a rainbow — and the 'Be Like Mike' era welded it to basketball royalty forever. Still sold everywhere; what's archived here is the decade it became a culture.
