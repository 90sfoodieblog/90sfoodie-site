Doritos 3D

The puffed triangle that inflated the Dorito.

It came back!
MakerFrito-Lay
Years1998–2004 · back 2020
StatusBrought back
ShelfChips & Snacks

In 1998, Frito-Lay looked at the flat triangle and asked: what if it had air in it? Doritos 3D were puffed, hollow, three-dimensional shells — fans still describe them as Doritos-meets-Bugles — with an airy crunch that gave way to the full seasoned hit. Nacho Cheese, Zesty Ranch, Jalapeño Cheddar, Cool Ranch: lunchbox royalty, cafeteria currency, the crinkle of the bag alone turned heads.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

They vanished around 2004, and the fans never shut up about it — petitions, forum threads, two decades of noise. In late 2020 Frito-Lay finally answered, relaunching them as Doritos 3D Crunch with the original 'Doritos Girl,' Ali Landry, back for the campaign. Some comebacks are marketing. This one felt like justice.
