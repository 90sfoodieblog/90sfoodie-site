Wendy's Superbar

An all-you-can-eat buffet inside a fast food joint. It was real.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerWendy's
Years1980s–1990s
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfFast Food

Yes, it was real. The Superbar was Wendy's short-lived answer to the sit-down buffet, glowing under heat lamps in the middle of a fast food joint. Three stations for a few bucks, all you could eat: the Garden Spot salad bar, a pasta station with spaghetti, alfredo, and marinara, and Mexican Fiesta — build-your-own tacos with seasoned beef, beans, salsa, and cheese. A choose-your-own-adventure with a tray.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The thrill was the slide of the tray along that shiny metal rail, plastic plate in hand, eyes wide. You'd start with salad to 'balance things out,' then pile spaghetti and taco meat onto the same tray and call it dinner. A little chaotic, not exactly gourmet — and completely special. Nothing in fast food has ever felt quite that generous since.
