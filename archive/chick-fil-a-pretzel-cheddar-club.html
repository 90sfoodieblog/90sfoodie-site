Chick-fil-A Pretzel Cheddar Club

A buttery pretzel bun, sharp cheddar, and that signature filet.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerChick-fil-A
YearsLimited-time favorite
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfFast Food

Some sandwiches hit every note at once, and the Pretzel Cheddar Club was one of them: a warm, buttery pretzel bun — soft inside, with that classic chewy, salty crust — holding Chick-fil-A's hand-breaded, pressure-cooked chicken filet and a blanket of melted sharp cheddar. Bold, hearty, and built like the great lunch-hour sandwiches of the 90s.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The cheddar brought the tang and the cheese pull; the pretzel bun brought a salty-sweet chew no regular bun could match. It carried an unmistakable throwback energy — comfort food done right — and like all the best limited runs, it left fans checking the menu board hoping it wandered back.
