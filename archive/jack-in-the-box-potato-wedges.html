Jack in the Box Bacon Cheddar Potato Wedges

Crinkle wedges drowned in cheese sauce, bacon, and green onion.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerJack in the Box
YearsMid-to-late 1990s
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfFast Food

Before loaded fries were a menu category, Jack in the Box was drowning crinkle-cut potato wedges in warm cheddar cheese sauce, showering them with bacon, and finishing with green onion. Simple arithmetic, perfect result: crispy wedge, molten cheese, salty bacon crunch in every forkful.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

Our blog post rebuilds them at home — wedges baked or air-fried until crisp, cheese sauce warmed, toppings scattered, ideally served in a red cardboard tray with a plastic fork for max realism. Some sides were never really sides. These were the main event wearing a side's name tag.
