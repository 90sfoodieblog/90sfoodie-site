The story

Kellogg's took the campfire's greatest hit — graham, chocolate, marshmallow — and rebuilt it as a breakfast: chocolate-flavored graham pieces with marshmallows swimming in milk. Smorz was dessert logic applied to the cereal aisle, and it worked beautifully.

It ran from 2003 to 2013, a 2000s guest in this archive, and its fans have been asking for a comeback ever since. Some cereals are breakfasts. This one was a bonfire you could eat with a spoon on a school morning.
