Cow and Chicken Puffs

Cartoon Network's weirdest siblings, in a cereal box — sweet corn puffs, zero udder content.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerKellogg's
YearsLate 1990s–1999
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfCereals

Kellogg's turned Cartoon Network's weirdest siblings into breakfast: crunchy yellow and white puffs of sweetened popped corn, glazed with sugar, on a riot-of-orange box — Cow grinning that enormous grin, Chicken looking exactly as alarmed as Chicken always looked.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The show wrapped in 1999, the licensing faded, and the Puffs quietly disappeared from grocery shelves. No farewell announcement, no final marked-down box. One school year you were pouring them into a bowl; the next, they were gone — which is exactly why this archive treats boxes like this one as museum pieces.
