The story

The 90s were Pizza Hut's mad-scientist era — stuffed crusts, layered experiments, pizza engineered like architecture. The TripleDeckeroni was peak ambition: a multi-deck pepperoni construction that treated 'how many layers can a pizza have?' as a question worth answering in public.

It didn't survive the decade, which is exactly the point of this archive. The 90s fast-food wars produced glorious, excessive, limited-run inventions — and the TripleDeckeroni stacked itself straight into legend.
