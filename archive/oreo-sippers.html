Oreo Sippers

The cookie straw you drank your milk through.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerNabisco
Years2000s–2012
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfCandy

Most snacks you ate. Oreo Sippers you drank through first. Nabisco's wafer-cookie straws turned an ordinary glass of milk into an event: sip, and the milk came up through the cookie, softening it from the inside, until the straw itself became the best bite of the glass. Chocolate or Golden — you had a team, and you defended it.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

Sippers rode the edible-straw craze of the late 90s and 2000s, then quietly disappeared around 2012 — no announcement, no farewell tour. Fans petitioned; Nabisco's only answer was a hopeful 'never say never.' Japan's Oreo Wafer Rolls are the closest living relative, but they never doubled as a straw. The original multitasker is still waiting on its comeback.
