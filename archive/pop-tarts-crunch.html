Pop-Tarts Crunch

The 90s cereal that turned breakfast into dessert.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerKellogg's
Years1994–1995
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfCereals

In the mid-90s, Kellogg's looked at the Pop-Tart — the undisputed king of the toaster — and asked a dangerous question: what if you could eat it with a spoon? The answer was Pop-Tarts Crunch: everything kids loved about Frosted Strawberry Pop-Tarts, shrunk into crunchy, spoonable, milk-soakable bites.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

It ruled the cereal aisle for a brief, shining moment, then vanished after about a year — too sweet for parents, too perfect for kids, and gone before most of us had said a proper goodbye. Breakfast and dessert's beautiful baby, remembered here in full.
