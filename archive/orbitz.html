Orbitz Soda

The soda with floating orbs — a lava lamp you could drink.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerClearly Canadian
Years1997–1998
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfDrinks

Debuting in 1997 from Clearly Canadian, Orbitz wasn't just a soda — it was an event. A clear, fizzy fruit drink with colorful gel orbs suspended motionless throughout the bottle, like a lava lamp you could drink. Raspberry, citrus, grape: the flavors were good, but the floating spheres were the show, turning every bottle into a tiny hypnotic display.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The orbs were made of gellan, a seaweed-derived gel, and they gave each sip a chewy surprise. Plenty of people were skeptical; the curious were enchanted. Orbitz was gone within about a year — too weird to live, too wonderful to forget — and it remains the 90s drink that felt beamed in from a friendlier future.
