Pepsi Blue

The electric blue berry soda that defined 2002 — and vanished.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerPepsiCo
Years2002–2004 · brief return 2021
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfDrinks

In the summer of 2002, Pepsi did something no major cola had dared to do: it turned blue. Not blue-raspberry-candy blue — a deep, electric, almost glowing blue that looked beamed down from another planet. Pepsi Blue didn't look like soda. It looked like the future.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The berry cola burned bright and fast, gone from US shelves by 2004 — long enough to become a legend. It briefly returned in 2021 and vanished again, which somehow only made the myth stronger. A 2000s guest in this archive by popular demand, and the blue our whole look tips its hat to.
