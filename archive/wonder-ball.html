Wonder Ball

The chocolate treasure you had to crack open.

It came back!
MakerNestlé
Years1990s original · revived in the 2010s
StatusBrought back
ShelfCandy

If you were a kid in the 90s, you remember the ritual. You didn't just eat a Wonder Ball — you performed surgery on it. Hold the foil-wrapped sphere in your palm, crack the milk chocolate shell, and inside: a rattling treasure of tiny candy beads and, if the gods smiled, a miniature figurine staring back like you'd just hatched it.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The Nestlé Wonder Ball hit American shelves in the 1990s and became an instant checkout-line legend. Prizes later gave way to candy-only versions, the original disappeared — and the format was revived in the 2010s for a new generation of crack-it-open kids.
