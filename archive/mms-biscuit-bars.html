M&M's Biscuit Bars

The 90s treat America never got.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerMars
Years1990s · UK & beyond
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfCandy

Here's a 90s injustice in candy form: while American kids knew M&M's from bags and tubes, shoppers overseas got M&M's Biscuit Bars — crunchy biscuit layered with chocolate and studded with M&M's Minis. A candy bar and a cookie, engineered together, sold in shops an ocean away.

The story

It never got a real American release, which makes it the archive's favorite kind of entry: the one that sends readers down a 'wait, THAT existed?' rabbit hole. Some treasures you had to have a passport — or a pen pal — to taste.
