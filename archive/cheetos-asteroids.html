Cheetos Asteroids

Little Cheeto spheres in a can that doubled as a cup.

It came back!
MakerFrito-Lay
Years1990s–2000s · back 2019
StatusBrought back
ShelfChips & Snacks

If Doritos 3D were the headliner of Frito-Lay's canister era, Cheetos Asteroids were the cult favorite: little spheres of Cheeto — the Flamin' Hot ones especially — packed into a tall 'Go Snacks' tube. Pop the snap-on lid and the can became the cup. Packaging as ritual, and every kid understood it instantly.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The Asteroids burned bright and vanished with the rest of the tube era. But their fans stayed loud, and in 2019 Frito-Lay brought Flamin' Hot Asteroids back — announced by Chester Cheetah himself, in a rap video. When the mascot has to address the fans directly, you know the nostalgia runs deep.
