The Colossus

Jack in the Box's monument to appetite — two patties, zero restraint.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerJack in the Box
Years1990s
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfFast Food

The Colossus wasn't just a burger — it was a monument to appetite. Two thick patties stacked high with melted cheese, crispy bacon, lettuce that actually snapped, and a zesty sauce that landed right between smoky and sweet, all hugged by a toasted sesame bun that somehow held it together. Jack in the Box really outdid themselves with that one.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

Most memories of it live in weekend evenings: back seat of the car, windows down, burger in hand, fries rustling in the bag. The Colossus made fast food feel like an event — every bite a challenge and a reward. Menus are slicker and servings leaner now, which is exactly why this giant still looms so large.
