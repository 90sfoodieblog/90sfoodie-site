The story

Cinnamon Toast Crunch's maple-drenched sibling arrived in 1995 shaped like the world's smallest slices of French toast, each one dusted and syrup-sweet. It was breakfast cosplaying as breakfast, and kids were completely sold.

General Mills discontinued it in 2006 — and then the fans did something rare: they made enough noise that it actually came back in 2014. A discontinued cereal resurrected by pure nostalgia. This archive exists because that energy is real.
