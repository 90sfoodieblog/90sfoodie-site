Butterfinger Ice Cream Nuggets

Twenty poppable ice cream balls in a chocolate Butterfinger shell — sold by the tub.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerNestlé
YearsEarly 1990s · gone by the late 1990s
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfFrozen Treats

In the early 1990s, when candy bars were invading the freezer aisle, Nestlé hid what fans still call the greatest Butterfinger of all in a tub: twenty poppable balls of vanilla ice cream wrapped in a milk chocolate shell loaded with crispety, crunchety Butterfinger pieces. No stick, no bowl — fingers were the utensil, and nobody in history ate just one.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The tub made it a shareable freezer snack years before shareable was a marketing word, and it landed at peak Butterfinger mania. Then the crowded 90s freezer aisle did what it did to so many novelties: by the end of the decade the Nuggets were gone, and unlike the bar or the BB's, they never came back.
