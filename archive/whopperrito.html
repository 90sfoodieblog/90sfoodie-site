The story

In the summer of 2016, Burger King looked at its most iconic burger and asked a question nobody else dared to ask: what if the Whopper... was a burrito? The answer was the Whopperrito — every bit of flame-grilled Whopper goodness, wrapped tight in a warm flour tortilla.

It shouldn't have worked. It absolutely worked. And then, like all the best limited-time legends, it vanished — a fast-food fever dream that people still bring up unprompted. The archive keeps a seat for it: proof that the throwback spirit didn't retire with the 90s.
