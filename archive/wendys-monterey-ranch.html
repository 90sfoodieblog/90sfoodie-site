Wendy's Monterey Ranch Chicken Sandwich

Monterey Jack, bacon, and ranch — simple, and just right.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerWendy's
YearsEarly 1990s
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfFast Food

Long before fast food menus filled up with mashups and gimmicks, Wendy's quietly delivered one of its most beloved creations: the Monterey Ranch Chicken Sandwich. Grilled or crispy chicken, Monterey Jack cheese, crispy bacon, and a cool, creamy ranch sauce on a soft bun. It wasn't trying to be trendy. It just worked — the tang of the ranch, the mild richness of the cheese, just enough bacon for a smoky crunch.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

It gained a loyal following fast, and Wendy's brought it back a few times over the years by popular demand — but it never stayed for long. For everyone who tasted it back then, it remains one of the greats that disappeared too soon: proof that fast food exploring flavor didn't have to go overboard.
