Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup

The peanut butter cup with a cookie hiding inside.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerReese's
Years1994 · short run
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfCandy

Released in 1994, the Crunchy Cookie Cup answered a question nobody else thought to ask: what if a Reese's had a cookie inside it? The classic formula — smooth chocolate, creamy peanut butter — gained a layer of crunchy cookie pieces, and the texture math was perfect: creamy, crunchy, salty, sweet, all in one bite. No choosing between chocolate and cookies. It gave you both.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

It arrived right as 'cookie everything' mania swept the snack aisle — cookies in ice cream, in cereal, so why not in candy? The Crunchy Cookie Cup didn't last, but for a certain generation it remains the best version of the cup Reese's ever made, and the one they'd trade the standard for in a heartbeat.
