Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup

The peanut butter cup with a cookie hiding inside.

Gone, not forgotten
MakerReese's
Years1994 · short run
StatusDiscontinued
ShelfCandy

The 1990s were a golden age for snack food innovation. It was the decade where we saw snacks take on a whole new personality, pushing boundaries and combining flavors in ways that were bold, exciting, and — at times — borderline ridiculous. But one product, in particular, stood out not just because of its audacity but because it was the perfect blend of two things we could never get enough of: chocolate and cookies. Enter the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup. A snack that still holds a special place in our hearts.

The story

If you grew up in the 90s, you likely remember the introduction of the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup. It wasn't just a peanut butter cup — it was a next level peanut butter cup. Reese's had already mastered the art of combining creamy peanut butter and smooth chocolate, but they took it a step further by adding a layer of crunchy cookie bits inside the cup. Essentially, they created a perfect hybrid of your favorite chocolate snack and that irresistible, chewy-cookie crunch we all crave.

Released in 1994, the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup came at a time when consumers were getting more adventurous with their taste buds. Think of it as Reese's response to the growing trend of "cookie" everything — cookies in ice cream, cookies in cereal, cookies in candy. Why not cookies inside peanut butter cups? It was a genius move, turning a classic into a snack that felt fresh, exciting, and just the right amount of indulgent.

The true magic of the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup wasn't just in its combination of ingredients — it was the perfect balance of textures. We all know the classic Reese's Peanut Butter Cup, with its creamy, velvety peanut butter filling and smooth chocolate shell. It's a flawless bite every time. But when Reese's added the crunchy cookie layer, they gave us something even better.

The inside of the Crunchy Cookie Cup was a dreamy marriage of smooth, rich peanut butter and a layer of crispy, crunchy cookie pieces. Each bite gave you the satisfaction of that peanut butter and chocolate pairing we all know and love, plus a little extra crunch — which, let's be honest, always makes everything better. It was like they took our favorite chocolate chip cookies and mashed them together with Reese's, and somehow made it better.

It was a flavor combo that checked all the boxes: creamy, crunchy, salty, sweet. You didn't need to choose between chocolate or cookies because the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup gave you both at once.

Let's not mince words here: the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup was downright addictive. The balance of textures created a satisfying crunch, while the peanut butter was smooth and perfectly salted. Then, the chocolate shell added that familiar, comforting sweetness. And with every bite, you'd get a mix of the rich, peanut-buttery center and the delightful, crispy cookie pieces that just worked. They were indulgent without being overwhelming, and each bite was just enough to keep you reaching for more.

This was no mere candy bar — it was a snack that hit every note. It made you feel like you were having a treat, not just a quick sugar fix. The best part? It was portable. You could grab one, pop it in your bag, and enjoy it wherever you were. No mess, no fuss, just pure snacking bliss.

Unfortunately, as with so many great snacks from the 90s, the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup met an untimely end. By the late 90s, it had been pulled from the shelves, leaving a hole in our snack-loving hearts. Why? Perhaps it was a victim of the rapidly changing snack market, or maybe Reese's decided to return to the tried-and-true formula of the classic Peanut Butter Cup. But whatever the reason, the Crunchy Cookie Cup vanished without warning — and it was sorely missed.

Some fans even went so far as to try and create their own version of the beloved cup, but nothing could ever quite replicate the perfection of the original.

Even though it's no longer around, the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup holds a unique place in 90s food history. It was a snack that spoke to the era — playful, bold, and a little decadent. It was the candy that got you through study sessions, late-night snack attacks, and maybe even that sugar rush that kept you going during the weekend's all-night video game marathons.

The Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup lives on in the hearts of those who remember the joy of tearing open that little orange wrapper and biting into something that was just right. It was one of those snacks that didn't try to be anything it wasn't — it didn't need to be the healthiest or the fanciest, it just needed to deliver on what it promised: chocolate, peanut butter, and cookies.

As we dive deeper into the world of snack food mashups and limited-edition flavors, there's a small but passionate faction of '90s kids who still hold out hope for a Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup revival. After all, nostalgia is powerful, and the demand for retro flavors is stronger than ever. In an age of gourmet peanut butter cups and innovative candy collaborations, there's still room for the classics — especially one as beloved as the Crunchy Cookie Cup.

So, let's raise a glass to the Reese's Crunchy Cookie Cup: a 90s snack that made us believe there was no such thing as too much peanut butter, chocolate, or crunch. It may not be on shelves anymore, but the memories of its satisfying, cookie-filled bliss will live on in our hearts — and in our snack cravings.

Maybe, just maybe, it's time for a comeback. Until then, we'll savor the sweet memories of that perfect peanut butter cookie cup from the golden age of snack food.
