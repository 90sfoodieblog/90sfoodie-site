Grown-Up 90s Pepperoni Pizza

The blog's own recipe — Friday pizza night, upgraded but unmistakably 90s.

Not everything in the archive came from a factory. This one comes from the blog's own kitchen: a grown-up rebuild of the 90s pepperoni pizza — fresh dough stretched thin, San Marzano tomato sauce with garlic and oregano, low-moisture mozzarella layered with smoked provolone, and dry-aged pepperoni crisping at the edges in a ripping-hot oven.

Read the full story on the blog

The story

The upgrades stay honest to the original spirit: red onion, pickled peppers if you're feeling it, fresh basil, and a drizzle of hot honey at the end. It's the full recipe on the blog — the pizza night you remember, made the way you'd make it now. Seven to ten minutes at 500 degrees. Childhood, but with better cheese.
