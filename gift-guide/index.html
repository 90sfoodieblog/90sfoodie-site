90s Foodie Holiday Gift Guide

Gifts for the 90s food lover in your life — every pick comes straight from this archive. Some of these snacks are back on real shelves today; the rest survive as vintage finds, hunted secondhand the way all great discontinued treasure is. No prices listed here — they change; the shopping links carry the current number.
