Terms

What this is. 90s Foodie is an enthusiast archive of 1990s snacks, drinks, cereals, and fast food, published for nostalgia, documentation, and education. It is unaffiliated with the brands it remembers.

Names and marks. Product names, brand names, mascots, and slogans belong to their owners. They are used here in the nominative, descriptive sense — to identify and document the products — and their use implies no endorsement by, or affiliation with, any brand owner.

The record. Entries, quizzes, and trivia are drawn from the archive's documented fact bank and are believed accurate as published. History occasionally surfaces a correction; when a fact is shown to be wrong, the archive corrects the entry rather than defending the error.

Use. Contents are published for reading and for citation with credit to 90s Foodie. The text of the archive is its own work; quoting it with a link back is welcome, wholesale republication is not.
