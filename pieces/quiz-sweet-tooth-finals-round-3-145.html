The Answer Key

  1. B. Lemonhead — Ferrara, 1962. The lemon drop with the pucker face on the box.
  2. A. Hot Tamales — Just Born, 1950. Cinnamon heat in a red capsule — Mike and Ike's spicy shadow.
  3. B. Cry Baby — Cry Baby, 1991. Extra-sour gumballs sold as a toughness test.
  4. A. Tootsie Roll — Tootsie, 1896. The owl's question outlived the century.
  5. A. Atomic Fireballs — Ferrara, 1954. The cinnamon jawbreaker that separated the brave from the wise.
  6. B. Candy Necklaces — Candy Necklaces, early years. Wearable sugar beads, chalky and pastel, eaten off the string.
  7. D. Charms Blow Pop — Charms, 1973. A lollipop with gum at the center — two candies, one stick.
  8. A. Crispy M&M's — Mars, 1999–2005. The orange-shelled crispy M&M, gone from the US in 2005 and revived a decade later.
  9. D. Good & Plenty — Hershey, 1893. Licorice pellets in pink and white, shaken like a train by Choo Choo Charlie.
  10. B. Dubble Bubble — Tootsie, 1928. The first bubble gum, pink since the inventor's only dye was pink.
