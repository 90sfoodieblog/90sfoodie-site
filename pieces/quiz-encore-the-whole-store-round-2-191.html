Encore: The Whole Store, Round 2

Encore: The Whole Store, Round 2 deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in Encore: The Whole Store, Round 2, the record does the talking.

    A. Otter Pops
    B. Planters

    D. Chick-fil-A

The Answer Key

  1. A. Otter Pops — Otter Pops, 1970. Freeze-it-yourself juice bars with pun-named characters.
  2. B. Snickers — Mars, 1930. Named for the Mars family's horse; the 90s bar of choice at every checkout.
  3. C. Sprinkle Spangles — General Mills, 1993–1998. A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
  4. D. Barq's Root Beer — Barq's, 1898. The caffeinated root beer with bite, from Biloxi in 1898.
  5. D. Skor — Hershey, 1981. Thin butter toffee under milk chocolate, with a Swedish crown on the wrapper.
  6. A. Big Mac — McDonald's, 1968. The jingle lists the whole build; a Pittsburgh creation that went national in 1968.
  7. D. Trolli Gummi Worms — Trolli, 1981. Gummi worms crawled out of Germany in 1981 and into every 90s candy bin.
  8. B. Mallomars — Nabisco, 1913. Chocolate marshmallow cookies sold only in cool months, so the chocolate wouldn't melt.
  9. A. Spree — Nestlé, 1971. The candy-coated cousin of SweeTarts.
  10. B. SpaghettiOs — Campbell's, 1965. Franco-American's rings in tomato sauce, thermos-transported by millions.
