The Encore: The Whole Store, Round 3 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Encore: The Whole Store, Round 3 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Reese's Pieces — Hershey, 1978. E.T.'s candy in 1982 made it an icon; the 90s kept it there.
  2. A. Starburst — Mars, 1967. Fruit chews that reached America in 1967 as Starburst after a UK life as Opal Fruits.
  3. D. Kit Kat — Hershey, 1935. The break-apart bar with the jingle everyone can finish.
  4. C. Mistic — Mistic, 1990. Tropical glass-bottle teas and juices from the Snapple era shelf.
  5. C. Big Mac — McDonald's, 1968. The jingle lists the whole build; a Pittsburgh creation that went national in 1968.
  6. B. Keebler Fudge Stripes — Keebler, early years. Shortbread rings striped with fudge by elves in a hollow tree.
  7. D. Jell-O Pudding Snacks — Jell-O, 1980. Pudding pops' cup cousins in the refrigerated case.
  8. C. Sno Balls — Hostess, 1947. Pink marshmallow snowballs with coconut — the color was the flavor's whole identity.
  9. C. Haribo Goldbears — Haribo, 1922. The original gummi bears, from Bonn in 1922, in US stores by 1982.
  10. D. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
