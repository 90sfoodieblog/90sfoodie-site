The Answer Key

  1. C. Dairy Queen Blizzard — Dairy Queen, 1985. So thick it was served upside down — the flip was the proof.
  2. A. Super Size — McDonald's, 1987–2004. The upsell that defined an era, retired in 2004.
  3. C. McDonald's Fried Apple Pie — McDonald's, 1968–1992. The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
  4. A. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
  5. D. Chuck E. Cheese — Chuck E. Cheese, 1977. Founded by Atari's Nolan Bushnell; absorbed ShowBiz Pizza in the 90s and kept the mouse.
  6. C. KFC Chicken Littles — KFC, 1986–1990. Tiny chicken sandwiches for pocket change, gone too soon, revived decades later as Littles.
  7. C. Chicken McNuggets — McDonald's, 1983. Debuted nationally in 1983; the sauces were half the ritual.
  8. B. Sonic Drive-In — Sonic, 1953. Carhops on skates and a happy hour of half-price drinks.
  9. D. Chick-fil-A Chicken Sandwich — Chick-fil-A, 1964. Pressure-cooked with pickle chips; the mall food court's southern anchor.
  10. B. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
