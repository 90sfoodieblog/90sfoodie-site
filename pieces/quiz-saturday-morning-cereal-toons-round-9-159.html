Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 9

This is Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 9 — a ten-question round built entirely from documented shelf history. Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 9 asks no trick questions and repeats no rumors: the archive only asks what it can prove. The Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 9 key is printed at the end, the archive way.

The Answer Key

  1. D. Golden Crisp — Post, 1949. Sugar Bear crooned it, cool as a jazz singer.
  2. C. Lucky Charms — General Mills, 1964. The marshmallow count kept growing through the 90s — blue diamonds arrived in 1998.
  3. D. Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries — Quaker, 1967. The berry pieces turned the milk pink-purple.
  4. A. Honey Smacks — Kellogg's, 1953. Dig 'Em hopped onto the box in 1972; the cereal was Sugar Smacks before 1984.
  5. B. Ghostbusters Cereal — Ralston, 1985–1989. Ghost-shaped marshmallows and Slimer on the box.
  6. B. Kix — General Mills, 1937. The first puffed corn cereal, puffed in a gun-like machine.
  7. B. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
  8. C. Franken Berry — General Mills, 1971. The strawberry monster cereal, twin debut with Count Chocula.
  9. D. Nintendo Cereal System — Ralston, 1988–1989. Two cereals in one box — Super Mario fruit and Zelda berry, split down the middle.
  10. B. Golden Grahams — General Mills, 1976. Honey graham squares that anchored a decade of s'mores-flavored everything.
