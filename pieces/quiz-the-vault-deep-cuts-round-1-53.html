The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 1

The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 1 deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 1, the record does the talking.

  1. Who fronted Ecto Cooler in the ads?
    A. Sugar Bear
    B. Sailor Jack
    C. Slimer
    D. Scooby-Doo
  2. Name the product in this memory: “The upsell that defined an era, retired in 2004.”
    A. Super Size
    B. Bigfoot Pizza
    C. Hardee's Frisco Burger
    D. McPizza

The Answer Key

  1. C. Ecto Cooler — Hi-C, 1987–2001. The green tangerine juice box with Slimer on the front, outliving the cartoon by a decade.
  2. A. Super Size — McDonald's, 1987–2004. The upsell that defined an era, retired in 2004.
  3. C. McPizza — McDonald's, 1989–2000. McDonald's pizza slowed the drive-thru to a crawl; gone by 2000 in most stores.
  4. D. Oreo O's — Post, 1998–2007. Chocolate rings with marshmallow bits; left the US in 2007, returned a decade later.
  5. B. Oreo O's — Post, 1998–2007. Chocolate rings with marshmallow bits; left the US in 2007, returned a decade later.
  6. C. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
  7. B. Reptar Crunch — Post, 1999–1999. A limited Rugrats cereal tied to the first Rugrats movie.
  8. A. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  9. B. Taco Bell Chihuahua — Taco Bell, 1997–2000. Gidget the chihuahua was 1997's most quoted dog; she also wanted Godzilla tickets.
  10. C. Super Size — McDonald's, 1987–2004. The upsell that defined an era, retired in 2004.
