The Answer Key

  1. A. Pillsbury Doughboy — Pillsbury, 1961. Poppin' Fresh has giggled at a finger poke since 1961.
  2. B. Zingers — Hostess, early years. The raspberry-coconut and chocolate twins of the snack-cake shelf.
  3. C. Fig Newtons — Nabisco, 1891. Named for Newton, Massachusetts — not the physicist.
  4. C. Vienna Fingers — Keebler, early years. The oval vanilla sandwich cookie of the office tray.
  5. C. Zingers — Hostess, early years. The raspberry-coconut and chocolate twins of the snack-cake shelf.
  6. A. Little Debbie Star Crunch — Little Debbie, early years. The cosmic rice-crisp and caramel disc that pre-dated Cosmic Brownies.
  7. B. SnackWell's Devil's Food Cookie Cakes — Nabisco, 1992–2018. The fat-free era's emblem — the diet cookies people ate by the sleeve.
  8. A. Fig Newtons — Nabisco, 1891. Named for Newton, Massachusetts — not the physicist.
  9. C. Tastykake Butterscotch Krimpets — Tastykake, 1914. Philadelphia's sponge cakes with butterscotch icing, from 1914.
  10. C. Animal Crackers (Barnum's) — Nabisco, 1902. The circus-cage box with a string handle, a Christmas ornament by design.
