The Answer Key

  1. D. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
  2. B. King Vitaman — Quaker, 1970–2019. The crowned king of the value shelf, poured for nearly fifty years.
  3. C. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
  4. D. Fruity Yummy Mummy — General Mills, 1987–1992. The fifth monster cereal, gone before the decade turned.
  5. C. The Noid — Domino's, 1986–1995. The claymation villain who ruined pizzas — Domino's mascot of chaos.
  6. A. McDLT — McDonald's, 1984–1990. Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
  7. B. Sprinkle Spangles — General Mills, 1993–1998. A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
  8. D. McDonald's Fried Apple Pie — McDonald's, 1968–1992. The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
  9. D. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  10. D. Necco Wafers — Necco, 1847–2018. The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
