Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 2

Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 2: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 2 asks it, the file answered it first. In Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 2, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

  1. Which aisle of the 90s did Berry Berry Kix call home?
    A. Fast Food
    B. Snacks & Chips
    C. Gum
    D. Cereals
  2. Cocoa Pebbles came from Post. Which of these did too?
    A. Waffle Crisp
    B. Milk Duds
    C. Skittles
    D. Kix
  3. Name the product in this memory: “Bat-shaped cereal for Tim Burton's summer.”
    A. Honey Smacks
    B. Batman Cereal
    C. Peanut Butter Crunch
    D. Waffle Crisp
  4. Which product promised: “Breakfast of Champions”?
    A. M&M's Minis
    B. Snickers
    C. Wheaties
    D. SunnyD
  5. Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries came from Quaker. Which of these did too?
    A. Circus Peanuts
    B. Handi-Snacks
    C. Quisp
    D. Tato Skins
  6. Name the product in this memory: “The Cap'n stayed crunchy, even in milk — the box's eternal promise.”
    A. Morning Funnies
    B. Cap'n Crunch
    C. Reptar Crunch
    D. Berry Berry Kix
  7. In what year did Apple Jacks first reach shelves?
    A. 1966
    B. 1969
    C. 1960
    D. 1965
  8. Which debuted first — Honey Smacks, Wheaties, King Vitaman, or Frute Brute?
    A. Frute Brute
    B. Honey Smacks
    C. King Vitaman
    D. Wheaties
  9. Which debuted first — Golden Grahams, Fruity Yummy Mummy, Rice Krispies, or Ghostbusters Cereal?
    A. Golden Grahams
    B. Fruity Yummy Mummy
    C. Rice Krispies
    D. Ghostbusters Cereal
  10. Which of these vanished from the shelves — Big League Chew, Nerds, Pop-Tarts Crunch, or SpaghettiOs?
    A. Nerds
    B. Pop-Tarts Crunch
    C. SpaghettiOs
    D. Big League Chew

The Answer Key

  1. D. Berry Berry Kix — General Mills, 1992. Kix mixed with purple berry-flavored clusters.
  2. A. Waffle Crisp — Post, 1996. Mini waffle squares with a maple-syrup taste.
  3. B. Batman Cereal — Ralston, 1989–1990. Bat-shaped cereal for Tim Burton's summer.
  4. C. Wheaties — General Mills, 1924. The orange box put Michael Jordan on more breakfast tables than anyone.
  5. C. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  6. B. Cap'n Crunch — Quaker, 1963. The Cap'n stayed crunchy, even in milk — the box's eternal promise.
  7. D. Apple Jacks — Kellogg's, 1965. The ads' eternal mystery: they don't taste like apples, and the kids didn't care.
  8. D. Wheaties — General Mills, 1924. The orange box put Michael Jordan on more breakfast tables than anyone.
  9. C. Rice Krispies — Kellogg's, 1928. The elves' names are the sound of the cereal in milk.
  10. B. Pop-Tarts Crunch — Kellogg's, 1994–1995. The toaster pastry shrunk into a cereal, frosted strawberry and brown sugar cinnamon.
