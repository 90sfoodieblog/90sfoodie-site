Debut Decade: Late 90s Arrivals, Round 8

The Debut Decade: Late 90s Arrivals, Round 8 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Debut Decade: Late 90s Arrivals, Round 8 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

    A. Animal Crackers (Barnum's)
    B. Cosmic Brownies
    C. Little Debbie Swiss Rolls

    B. Fruitopia
    C. Uncrustables
    D. Bigfoot Pizza

The Answer Key

  1. B. Cosmic Brownies — Little Debbie, 1999. The rainbow-sprinkled brownie that closed the decade.
  2. C. Reptar Crunch — Post, 1999–1999. A limited Rugrats cereal tied to the first Rugrats movie.
  3. B. Waffle Crisp — Post, 1996. Mini waffle squares with a maple-syrup taste.
  4. D. Jack in the Box Antenna Balls — Jack in the Box, 1995. Jack's comeback era (from 1994) put his head on a million antennas.
  5. D. Combos — Mars, 1978. Cheese-filled pretzel and cracker tubes — road-trip fuel.
  6. D. Cosmic Brownies — Little Debbie, 1999. The rainbow-sprinkled brownie that closed the decade.
  7. C. Waffle Crisp — Post, 1996. Mini waffle squares with a maple-syrup taste.
  8. C. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
  9. B. Stuffed Crust Pizza — Pizza Hut, 1995. Cheese sealed in the crust — eat the pizza backwards, said the ads.
  10. C. Uncrustables — Smucker's, 1995. Sealed crustless PB&J, patented and freezer-born — the lunchbox shortcut of 1995.
