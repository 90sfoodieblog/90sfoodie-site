The Answer Key

  1. C. OK Soda — Coca-Cola, 1993–1995. Coke's Gen-X experiment with an underground-comics manifesto on every can.
  2. C. TCBY — TCBY, 1981. The frozen-yogurt era's flagship — soft serve swirls in every mall.
  3. C. Twizzlers — Hershey, 1929. The twisted strawberry standard since 1929.
  4. D. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  5. B. Cocoa Puffs — General Mills, 1958. Sonny went cuckoo in every 90s commercial break.
  6. B. Push Pop — Topps, 1986. Save some for later — the cap made it a promise kids broke daily.
  7. C. Surge — Coca-Cola, 1996–2003. Coke's citrus answer to Mountain Dew; fans campaigned it back in 2014.
  8. A. Wheaties — General Mills, 1924. The orange box put Michael Jordan on more breakfast tables than anyone.
  9. B. OK Soda — Coca-Cola, 1993–1995. Coke's Gen-X experiment with an underground-comics manifesto on every can.
  10. C. Trix — General Mills, 1954. Turned fruit-shaped in 1991, the decade's defining cereal-box silhouette.
