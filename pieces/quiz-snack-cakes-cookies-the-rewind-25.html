The Answer Key

  1. B. Teddy Grahams — Nabisco, 1988. Bear-shaped grahams that danced through 90s commercials in sunglasses.
  2. C. Mrs. Fields Cookies — Mrs. Fields, 1977. The mall cookie stand whose smell reached you two stores away.
  3. C. Oatmeal Creme Pies — Little Debbie, 1960. Little Debbie's original — two soft oatmeal cookies around creme.
  4. A. Mrs. Fields Cookies — Mrs. Fields, 1977. The mall cookie stand whose smell reached you two stores away.
  5. A. Cosmic Brownies — Little Debbie, 1999. The rainbow-sprinkled brownie that closed the decade.
  6. C. Zebra Cakes — Little Debbie, early years. Striped white-and-chocolate snack cakes.
  7. D. Pillsbury Doughboy — Pillsbury, 1961. Poppin' Fresh has giggled at a finger poke since 1961.
  8. D. Nilla Wafers — Nabisco, 1898. The vanilla wafer that banana pudding cannot live without.
  9. D. Twinkies — Hostess, 1930. The golden snack cake that survived even Hostess's 2012 shutdown, returning in 2013.
  10. D. Gushers — Betty Crocker, 1991. Fruit snacks with a liquid center that burst — heads turned into fruit in the ads. (Filed here by lunchbox logic.)
