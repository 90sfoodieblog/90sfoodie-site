The Answer Key

  1. A. Combos — Mars, 1978. Cheese-filled pretzel and cracker tubes — road-trip fuel.
  2. C. Bazooka — Topps, 1947. The gum wrapped in a tiny comic about Bazooka Joe and his eyepatch.
  3. D. Fudgsicle — Popsicle, 1920. The fudge pop of a thousand summer afternoons.
  4. D. Hostess CupCakes — Hostess, 1919. Chocolate cake, cream filling, and the white squiggle.
  5. D. Hot Tamales — Just Born, 1950. Cinnamon heat in a red capsule — Mike and Ike's spicy shadow.
  6. A. Red Vines — American Licorice, 1952. West Coast red licorice, rival to the twist from the East.
  7. B. Frute Brute — General Mills, 1974–1982. The werewolf's fruit cereal, retired in the 80s and revived for Halloween seasons.
  8. B. Mr. Pibb — Coca-Cola, 1972–2001. The Dr Pepper rival renamed Pibb Xtra in 2001.
  9. A. Dots — Tootsie, 1945. Gumdrops in the yellow box, straight from the theater counter.
  10. D. Junior Mints — Tootsie, 1949. The movie-theater mint, Seinfeld's operating-room candy in 1993.
