Trivia Night: Encore: The Whole Store, Round 9

The Encore: The Whole Store, Round 9 night runs fifteen deep: rapid-fire questions from the shelf's own history, answers underneath with the receipts attached. Warm up the reading voice for Encore: The Whole Store, Round 9 — these are meant to be said out loud.

QuestionAnswer & the story
Which came first, Froot Loops or Oreo O's?Froot Loops — 1963, beating Oreo O's (1998) by 35 year(s).
Toucan Sam followed his nose; the loops were all one flavor despite the colors.
Doritos came from Frito-Lay. Name one more from the Frito-Lay shelf.Tostitos
Restaurant-style tortilla chips; Scoops! turned them into edible spoons in the 90s.
Which came first, Mary Jane or Kix?Mary Jane — 1914, beating Kix (1937) by 23 year(s).
Peanut butter and molasses chews in the yellow wrapper.
TMNT Pudding Pies came from Hostess. Name one more from the Hostess shelf.Zingers
The raspberry-coconut and chocolate twins of the snack-cake shelf.
Which came first, Uncrustables or Snickers?Snickers — 1930, beating Uncrustables (1995) by 65 year(s).
Named for the Mars family's horse; the 90s bar of choice at every checkout.
Which came first, Ben & Jerry's or Cocoa Puffs?Cocoa Puffs — 1958, beating Ben & Jerry's (1978) by 20 year(s).
Sonny went cuckoo in every 90s commercial break.
Which came first, Urkel-O's or Ben & Jerry's?Ben & Jerry's — 1978, beating Urkel-O's (1991) by 13 year(s).
Vermont's finest; Cherry Garcia (1987) and Phish Food (1997) were the decade's pints.
Which came first, Nilla Wafers or Arby's?Nilla Wafers — 1898, beating Arby's (1964) by 66 year(s).
The vanilla wafer that banana pudding cannot live without.
Ho Hos came from Hostess. Which other product did Hostess give the decade?Zingers
The raspberry-coconut and chocolate twins of the snack-cake shelf.
Which came first, Popsicle or Tongue Splashers?Popsicle — 1905, beating Tongue Splashers (1990) by 85 year(s).
Invented by an 11-year-old who left a soda out in the cold; twin pops split for sharing.
Which came first, Golden Grahams or Tato Skins?Golden Grahams — 1976, beating Tato Skins (1985) by 9 year(s).
Honey graham squares that anchored a decade of s'mores-flavored everything.
Chicken McNuggets came from McDonald's. Name one more from the McDonald's shelf.McDonald's Fried Apple Pie
The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
Which came first, Squeezit or Burger Bundles?Squeezit — 1985, beating Burger Bundles (1987) by 2 year(s).
Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
Which came first, Shark Bites or KFC Chicken Littles?KFC Chicken Littles — 1986, beating Shark Bites (1988) by 2 year(s).
Tiny chicken sandwiches for pocket change, gone too soon, revived decades later as Littles.
Ho Hos came from Hostess. Name one more from the Hostess shelf.Twinkies
The golden snack cake that survived even Hostess's 2012 shutdown, returning in 2013.
