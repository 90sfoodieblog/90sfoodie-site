The Answer Key

  1. D. French Toast Crunch — General Mills, 1995–2006. Tiny toast-shaped pieces in maple syrup flavor; fans won a revival in 2014.
  2. A. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  3. C. Bill & Ted's Excellent Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1991. A most excellent tie-in, gone as fast as the phone booth.
  4. C. The Noid — Domino's, 1986–1995. The claymation villain who ruined pizzas — Domino's mascot of chaos.
  5. A. SnackWell's Devil's Food Cookie Cakes — Nabisco, 1992–2018. The fat-free era's emblem — the diet cookies people ate by the sleeve.
  6. D. Sprinkle Spangles — General Mills, 1993–1998. A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
  7. A. Gushers — Betty Crocker, 1991. Fruit snacks with a liquid center that burst — heads turned into fruit in the ads. (Filed here by lunchbox logic.)
  8. A. Wendy's SuperBar — Wendy's, 1980–1998. The all-you-can-eat salad, pasta, and taco bar inside Wendy's — a buffet time capsule.
  9. D. McDonald's Fried Apple Pie — McDonald's, 1968–1992. The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
  10. D. Ecto Cooler — Hi-C, 1987–2001. The green tangerine juice box with Slimer on the front, outliving the cartoon by a decade.
