The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 8

The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 8: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 8 asks it, the file answered it first. In The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 8, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

The Answer Key

  1. B. Whatchamacallit — Hershey, 1978. The bar named for the word you use when you can't remember the word.
  2. C. Whatchamacallit — Hershey, 1978. The bar named for the word you use when you can't remember the word.
  3. C. Funyuns — Frito-Lay, 1969. Onion-flavored rings that taste nothing like an onion and everything like a Funyun.
  4. A. Kool-Aid Bursts — Kraft, 1994. Twist-top bottles of Kool-Aid for the lunchbox run, from 1994.
  5. A. Hot Pockets — Hot Pockets, 1983. The microwaveable turnover with the crisping sleeve sleight of hand.
  6. A. Chipwich — Chipwich, 1981. Two chocolate chip cookies, vanilla ice cream, rolled in chips.
  7. C. Choco Taco — Klondike, 1983–2022. The taco-shaped ice cream, mourned nationwide when Klondike retired it in 2022.
  8. B. Necco Wafers — Necco, 1847–2018. The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
  9. D. The Addams Family Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1992. Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
  10. C. Dubble Bubble — Tootsie, 1928. The first bubble gum, pink since the inventor's only dye was pink.
