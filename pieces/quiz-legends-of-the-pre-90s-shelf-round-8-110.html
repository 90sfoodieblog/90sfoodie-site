Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 8

Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 8: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 8 asks it, the file answered it first. In Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 8, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Doritos — Frito-Lay, 1966. Born at Disneyland's Casa de Fritos; Nacho Cheese arrived in 1972.
  2. A. Cracker Jack — Frito-Lay, 1896. Caramel corn and peanuts with a prize in every box since 1912.
  3. A. Mrs. Fields Cookies — Mrs. Fields, 1977. The mall cookie stand whose smell reached you two stores away.
  4. D. Hydrox — Hydrox, 1908–2015. The original chocolate sandwich cookie — Oreo was the copycat, in 1912.
  5. D. Snack Pack Pudding — Hunt's, 1968. The pudding cup of Billy Madison fame; the tab-top lid was a hazard and a rite.
  6. A. Milk Duds — Hershey, 1926. Caramel 'duds' — named for the round shape the machine couldn't make.
  7. D. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
  8. A. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  9. C. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  10. B. Popsicle — Popsicle, 1905. Invented by an 11-year-old who left a soda out in the cold; twin pops split for sharing.
