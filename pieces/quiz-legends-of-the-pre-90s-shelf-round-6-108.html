Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 6

The Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 6 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 6 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Ding Dongs — Hostess, 1967. A chocolate hockey puck of cake and cream.
  2. C. Reese's Pieces — Hershey, 1978. E.T.'s candy in 1982 made it an icon; the 90s kept it there.
  3. B. SpaghettiOs — Campbell's, 1965. Franco-American's rings in tomato sauce, thermos-transported by millions.
  4. B. Necco Wafers — Necco, 1847–2018. The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
  5. C. Bubble Yum — Bubble Yum, 1975. The soft bubble gum that fought off a spider-egg urban legend.
  6. D. Apple Jacks — Kellogg's, 1965. The ads' eternal mystery: they don't taste like apples, and the kids didn't care.
  7. A. Dubble Bubble — Tootsie, 1928. The first bubble gum, pink since the inventor's only dye was pink.
  8. D. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  9. C. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
  10. C. Faygo — Faygo, 1907. Detroit's soda — Rock & Rye — and the Insane Clown Posse's eternal spray.
