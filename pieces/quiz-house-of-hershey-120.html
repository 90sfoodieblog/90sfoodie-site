House of Hershey

The House of Hershey round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In House of Hershey the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

  1. In what year did Whoppers first reach shelves?
    A. 1959
    B. 1914
    C. 1935
    D. 1939
  2. Whatchamacallit came from Hershey. Which of these did too?
    A. Warheads
    B. Waffle Crisp
    C. Reese's Pieces
    D. Cinnamon Toast Crunch
  3. In what year did Twizzlers first reach shelves?
    A. 1931
    B. 1925
    C. 1926
    D. 1929
  4. Which aisle of the 90s did Kit Kat call home?
    A. Snacks & Chips
    B. Gum
    C. Candy
    D. Lunchbox

    A. Everlasting Gobstopper
    B. Whoppers

    D. Dum Dums
  6. Which aisle of the 90s did Whatchamacallit call home?
    A. Frozen & Ice Cream
    B. Gum

    D. Cereals

    A. Snickers
    B. Runts
    C. Butterfinger BB's
    D. Good & Plenty
  8. Which debuted first — Whoppers, Hershey's Cookies 'n' Creme, Twizzlers, or Milk Duds?
    A. Milk Duds
    B. Whoppers
    C. Twizzlers
    D. Hershey's Cookies 'n' Creme
  9. Which debuted first — Reese's Pieces, Kit Kat, Twizzlers, or Milk Duds?
    A. Kit Kat
    B. Reese's Pieces
    C. Milk Duds
    D. Twizzlers
  10. Which of these can you still buy today — Hershey's Cookies 'n' Creme, Pepsi A.M., Planters Cheez Balls, or Sprinkle Spangles?
    A. Sprinkle Spangles
    B. Hershey's Cookies 'n' Creme
    C. Planters Cheez Balls
    D. Pepsi A.M.

The Answer Key

  1. D. Whoppers — Hershey, 1939. Malted milk balls; at Easter they hatch as Robin Eggs.
  2. C. Reese's Pieces — Hershey, 1978. E.T.'s candy in 1982 made it an icon; the 90s kept it there.
  3. D. Twizzlers — Hershey, 1929. The twisted strawberry standard since 1929.
  4. C. Kit Kat — Hershey, 1935. The break-apart bar with the jingle everyone can finish.
  5. B. Whoppers — Hershey, 1939. Malted milk balls; at Easter they hatch as Robin Eggs.
  6. C. Whatchamacallit — Hershey, 1978. The bar named for the word you use when you can't remember the word.
  7. D. Good & Plenty — Hershey, 1893. Licorice pellets in pink and white, shaken like a train by Choo Choo Charlie.
  8. A. Milk Duds — Hershey, 1926. Caramel 'duds' — named for the round shape the machine couldn't make.
  9. C. Milk Duds — Hershey, 1926. Caramel 'duds' — named for the round shape the machine couldn't make.
  10. B. Hershey's Cookies 'n' Creme — Hershey, 1994. White creme and cookie bits — the bar that broke the brown-chocolate mold.
