The Answer Key

  1. D. Drumstick — Nestlé, 1928. The cone with the chocolate nugget at the bottom — the reward for finishing.
  2. C. TCBY — TCBY, 1981. The frozen-yogurt era's flagship — soft serve swirls in every mall.
  3. B. TCBY — TCBY, 1981. The frozen-yogurt era's flagship — soft serve swirls in every mall.
  4. C. Wendy's SuperBar — Wendy's, 1980–1998. The all-you-can-eat salad, pasta, and taco bar inside Wendy's — a buffet time capsule.
  5. C. Book It! — Pizza Hut, 1984. Pizza Hut's reading program: read books, earn a personal pan pizza — the 90s' tastiest homework.
  6. B. TCBY — TCBY, 1981. The frozen-yogurt era's flagship — soft serve swirls in every mall.
  7. D. Little Caesars Pizza!Pizza! — Little Caesars, 1979. Two pizzas for the price of one, in the long double box on paper.
  8. D. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  9. C. Popsicle — Popsicle, 1905. Invented by an 11-year-old who left a soda out in the cold; twin pops split for sharing.
  10. A. Totino's Pizza Rolls — Totino's, 1968. The molten pocket squares — patience was the only safe temperature.
