The Answer Key

  1. D. Super Size — McDonald's, 1987–2004. The upsell that defined an era, retired in 2004.
  2. C. Super Size — McDonald's, 1987–2004. The upsell that defined an era, retired in 2004.
  3. A. Wendy's Frosty — Wendy's, 1969. Too thick for a straw, too thin for a spoon — fries dipped in it are the law.
  4. C. Chick-fil-A Chicken Sandwich — Chick-fil-A, 1964. Pressure-cooked with pickle chips; the mall food court's southern anchor.
  5. D. McDLT — McDonald's, 1984–1990. Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
  6. D. Gushers — Betty Crocker, 1991. Fruit snacks with a liquid center that burst — heads turned into fruit in the ads. (Filed here by lunchbox logic.)
  7. D. Chick-fil-A Chicken Sandwich — Chick-fil-A, 1964. Pressure-cooked with pickle chips; the mall food court's southern anchor.
  8. B. Little Caesars Pizza!Pizza! — Little Caesars, 1979. Two pizzas for the price of one, in the long double box on paper.
  9. A. Book It! — Pizza Hut, 1984. Pizza Hut's reading program: read books, earn a personal pan pizza — the 90s' tastiest homework.
  10. D. McDonald's Fried Apple Pie — McDonald's, 1968–1992. The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
