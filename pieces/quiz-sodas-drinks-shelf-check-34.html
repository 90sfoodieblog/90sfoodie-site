The Answer Key

  1. A. Fruitopia Fruit Integration — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Fruitopia's flagship flavor name, peak 90s wordplay in a bottle.
  2. B. Crystal Pepsi — Pepsi, 1992–1994. The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
  3. C. Mondo — Jel Sert, 1991. Squeezable fruit drinks in the Mondo bottle, a lunchbox currency.
  4. B. Cherry Coke — Coca-Cola, 1985. Cherry Coke's 90s cans went full psychedelic black-cherry art.
  5. D. Kool-Aid Bursts — Kraft, 1994. Twist-top bottles of Kool-Aid for the lunchbox run, from 1994.
  6. C. Sierra Mist — Pepsi, 1999–2023. Pepsi's lemon-lime launch at the decade's edge.
  7. C. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
  8. B. Brisk Iced Tea — Lipton, 1991. Claymation celebrity ads made Brisk a 90s catchphrase machine.
  9. D. Faygo — Faygo, 1907. Detroit's soda — Rock & Rye — and the Insane Clown Posse's eternal spray.
  10. B. Faygo — Faygo, 1907. Detroit's soda — Rock & Rye — and the Insane Clown Posse's eternal spray.
