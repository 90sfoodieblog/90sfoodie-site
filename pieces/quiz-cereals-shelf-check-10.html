The Answer Key

  1. A. S'mores Crunch — General Mills, 1982–1988. Graham squares, chocolate, and marshmallow — returned in spirit as Smorz in 2003.
  2. B. Morning Funnies — Ralston, 1988–1989. A cereal box printed with Sunday comics panels.
  3. C. Urkel-O's — Ralston, 1991–1992. A Family Matters cereal — did it do that? It did, briefly.
  4. C. Urkel-O's — Ralston, 1991–1992. A Family Matters cereal — did it do that? It did, briefly.
  5. D. Honey Nut Cheerios — General Mills, 1979. The honey-nut bowl that outsold the original.
  6. C. Peanut Butter Crunch — Quaker, 1969. The peanut butter entry in the Cap'n's fleet.
  7. C. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
  8. A. Urkel-O's — Ralston, 1991–1992. A Family Matters cereal — did it do that? It did, briefly.
  9. C. Berry Berry Kix — General Mills, 1992. Kix mixed with purple berry-flavored clusters.
  10. D. Lucky Charms — General Mills, 1964. The marshmallow count kept growing through the 90s — blue diamonds arrived in 1998.
