Trivia Night: Encore: The Whole Store, Round 10

Fifteen rapid-fire questions for Encore: The Whole Store, Round 10, answers face-up underneath — this is the written kind of trivia night, the archive's way. Read the Encore: The Whole Store, Round 10 questions out loud for full effect.

QuestionAnswer & the story
McDonald's Happy Meal came from McDonald's. Which other product did McDonald's give the decade?McDonald's Fried Apple Pie
The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
Ding Dongs came from Hostess. What else did Hostess put on the shelves?Zingers
The raspberry-coconut and chocolate twins of the snack-cake shelf.
Which came first, Sno Balls or Taco Bell Chihuahua?Sno Balls — 1947, beating Taco Bell Chihuahua (1997) by 50 year(s).
Pink marshmallow snowballs with coconut — the color was the flavor's whole identity.
Which came first, Dairy Queen Blizzard or Bubble Jug?Dairy Queen Blizzard — 1985, beating Bubble Jug (1990) by 5 year(s).
So thick it was served upside down — the flip was the proof.
Which came first, Nilla Wafers or Kix?Nilla Wafers — 1898, beating Kix (1937) by 39 year(s).
The vanilla wafer that banana pudding cannot live without.
Nutty Bars came from Little Debbie. Which other product did Little Debbie give the decade?Little Debbie Swiss Rolls
The budget pinwheel that filled more lunchboxes than any rival.
Which came first, Cooler Ranch Doritos or Capri Sun?Capri Sun — 1981, beating Cooler Ranch Doritos (1986) by 5 year(s).
The silver pouch and the straw-stab ritual of every lunchbox.
Which came first, Pringles or Fruitopia Fruit Integration?Pringles — 1968, beating Fruitopia Fruit Integration (1994) by 26 year(s).
Chips engineered as identical saddles, stacked in a can to never break. The slogan debuted in 1994.
Which came first, Mondo or Slim Jim?Slim Jim — 1929, beating Mondo (1991) by 62 year(s).
The Macho Man years made 'snap into it' a catchphrase — oh yeah.
Which came first, Pixy Stix or Andy Capp's Hot Fries?Pixy Stix — 1952, beating Andy Capp's Hot Fries (1971) by 19 year(s).
Powdered sugar in a paper straw — pure sugar, poured.
Kraft Macaroni & Cheese came from Kraft. Name one more from the Kraft shelf.Kool-Aid Bursts
Twist-top bottles of Kool-Aid for the lunchbox run, from 1994.
Which came first, Nutty Bars or McDLT?Nutty Bars — 1964, beating McDLT (1984) by 20 year(s).
Peanut butter wafers in chocolate, sold as a two-stick team.
Which came first, Everlasting Gobstopper or Rice Krispies?Rice Krispies — 1928, beating Everlasting Gobstopper (1976) by 48 year(s).
The elves' names are the sound of the cereal in milk.
Which came first, Brisk Iced Tea or Morning Funnies?Morning Funnies — 1988, beating Brisk Iced Tea (1991) by 3 year(s).
A cereal box printed with Sunday comics panels.
Which came first, Doritos 3D's or Fruit by the Foot?Fruit by the Foot — 1991, beating Doritos 3D's (1998) by 7 year(s).
Three feet of fruit snack, unrolled like a measuring tape.
