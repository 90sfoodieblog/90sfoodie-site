Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 1

Ten questions on Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 1 — every one drawn from the archive's own record, every answer checked against the shelf it came from. The Saturday Morning: Cereal & Toons, Round 1 key waits at the bottom; no peeking, that's the rule of the rewind.

    A. Crispy
    B. Yummy Mummy
    C. Sugar Bear
    D. Debbi Fields
  2. Who fronted Ghostbusters Cereal in the ads?
    A. Chuck E. Cheese
    B. K.C. the Penguin

    D. Slimer

The Answer Key

  1. B. Fruity Yummy Mummy — General Mills, 1987–1992. The fifth monster cereal, gone before the decade turned.
  2. D. Ghostbusters Cereal — Ralston, 1985–1989. Ghost-shaped marshmallows and Slimer on the box.
  3. B. Count Chocula — General Mills, 1971. The chocolate monster cereal, a Halloween-season staple.
  4. B. Cookie Crisp — Ralston, 1977. The cereal that promised cookies for breakfast, sold by a wizard, then a burglar.
  5. B. Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries — Quaker, 1967. The berry pieces turned the milk pink-purple.
  6. B. The Addams Family Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1992. Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
  7. D. Frosted Flakes — Kellogg's, 1952. Tony's been roaring since 1952.
  8. B. Cocoa Puffs — General Mills, 1958. Sonny went cuckoo in every 90s commercial break.
  9. C. Golden Grahams — General Mills, 1976. Honey graham squares that anchored a decade of s'mores-flavored everything.
  10. D. Kix — General Mills, 1937. The first puffed corn cereal, puffed in a gun-like machine.
