The Answer Key

  1. D. Urkel-O's — Ralston, 1991–1992. A Family Matters cereal — did it do that? It did, briefly.
  2. D. McDLT — McDonald's, 1984–1990. Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
  3. A. Surge — Coca-Cola, 1996–2003. Coke's citrus answer to Mountain Dew; fans campaigned it back in 2014.
  4. B. Fruitopia Fruit Integration — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Fruitopia's flagship flavor name, peak 90s wordplay in a bottle.
  5. A. Keebler Magic Middles — Keebler, 1988–1995. Shortbread cookies with fudge middles — filed here by vending-machine logic.
  6. D. Hydrox — Hydrox, 1908–2015. The original chocolate sandwich cookie — Oreo was the copycat, in 1912.
  7. A. Pepsi Wild Cherry — Pepsi, 1988. Tested as Wild Cherry Pepsi from 1988; the cherry cola wars' survivor.
  8. A. Nintendo Cereal System — Ralston, 1988–1989. Two cereals in one box — Super Mario fruit and Zelda berry, split down the middle.
  9. D. Frute Brute — General Mills, 1974–1982. The werewolf's fruit cereal, retired in the 80s and revived for Halloween seasons.
  10. D. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
