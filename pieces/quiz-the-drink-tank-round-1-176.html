The Drink Tank, Round 1

The Drink Tank, Round 1: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if The Drink Tank, Round 1 asks it, the file answered it first. In The Drink Tank, Round 1, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

The Answer Key

  1. D. Pepsi Wild Cherry — Pepsi, 1988. Tested as Wild Cherry Pepsi from 1988; the cherry cola wars' survivor.
  2. B. SunnyD — Sunny Delight, 1963. The orange drink kids pulled from the fridge while parents reached for 'the purple stuff' (in the famous ad, the sunny one won).
  3. B. Squeezit — Betty Crocker, 1985–2001. Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
  4. C. New Coke — Coca-Cola, 1985–1985. The 79-day reformulation fiasco that made Coca-Cola Classic a comeback story.
  5. B. Gatorade — Pepsico, 1965. The 1991 'Be Like Mike' jingle made sports drink a Michael Jordan anthem.
  6. B. Hi-C Juice Boxes — Hi-C, 1955. The juice box standard; Flashin' Fruit Punch stained a generation of shirts.
  7. C. Barq's Root Beer — Barq's, 1898. The caffeinated root beer with bite, from Biloxi in 1898.
  8. C. Josta — Pepsi, 1995–1999. Pepsi's guarana soda — the first energy drink from a major soda company.
  9. D. Bug Juice — Bug Juice, 1990. The summer-camp canteen staple in the bug-logo bottle.
  10. B. Tampico — Tampico, 1989. Gallon citrus punch — the cookout and corner-store staple from 1989.
