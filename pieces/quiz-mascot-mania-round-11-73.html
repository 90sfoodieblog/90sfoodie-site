The Answer Key

  1. C. Flamin' Hot Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1990. The spicy legend of the snack aisle — red fingers were a badge of honor.
  2. C. Taco Bell Chihuahua — Taco Bell, 1997–2000. Gidget the chihuahua was 1997's most quoted dog; she also wanted Godzilla tickets.
  3. A. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  4. D. Zebra Cakes — Little Debbie, early years. Striped white-and-chocolate snack cakes.
  5. D. Twizzlers — Hershey, 1929. The twisted strawberry standard since 1929.
  6. B. Otter Pops — Otter Pops, 1970. Freeze-it-yourself juice bars with pun-named characters.
  7. D. Cinnamon Toast Crunch — General Mills, 1984. Cinnamon-sugar squares; Crazy Squares in the modern ads.
  8. B. Andy Capp's Hot Fries — Andy Capp's, 1971. Fry-shaped corn snacks with a comic-strip namesake.
  9. A. Froot Loops — Kellogg's, 1963. Toucan Sam followed his nose; the loops were all one flavor despite the colors.
  10. C. Fruity Yummy Mummy — General Mills, 1987–1992. The fifth monster cereal, gone before the decade turned.
