The Vault, Round 1: Gone But Not Forgotten

The Vault, Round 1: Gone But Not Forgotten deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in The Vault, Round 1: Gone But Not Forgotten, the record does the talking.

    A. Frozen & Ice Cream
    B. Sodas & Drinks
    C. Cereals
    D. Gum

The Answer Key

  1. C. Ghostbusters Cereal — Ralston, 1985–1989. Ghost-shaped marshmallows and Slimer on the box.
  2. B. Starburst — Mars, 1967. Fruit chews that reached America in 1967 as Starburst after a UK life as Opal Fruits.
  3. C. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
  4. C. Doritos 3D's — Frito-Lay, 1998–2004. Puffed 3D Doritos in a plastic canister — the decade's chip engineering marvel.
  5. C. Squeezit — Betty Crocker, 1985–2001. Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
  6. B. Burger King Kids Club — Burger King, 1990–2000. The Kids Club gang — Kid Vid, Boomer, I.Q., Jaws, and Snaps — on every box and cup.
  7. D. Squeezit — Betty Crocker, 1985–2001. Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
  8. B. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
  9. B. McPizza — McDonald's, 1989–2000. McDonald's pizza slowed the drive-thru to a crawl; gone by 2000 in most stores.
  10. C. Slice — Pepsi, 1984–2009. Pepsi's juice-blended soda line; its lemon-lime handed the torch to Sierra Mist in 1999.
