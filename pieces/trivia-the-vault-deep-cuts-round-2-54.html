Trivia Night: The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 2

The The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 2 night runs fifteen deep: rapid-fire questions from the shelf's own history, answers underneath with the receipts attached. Warm up the reading voice for The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 2 — these are meant to be said out loud.

QuestionAnswer & the story
True or false: Dunkaroos is still sold today.False — it left shelves in 2012.
Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
Which company made KFC Chicken Littles?KFC
Tiny chicken sandwiches for pocket change, gone too soon, revived decades later as Littles.
What year did Choco Taco debut?1983
The taco-shaped ice cream, mourned nationwide when Klondike retired it in 2022.
What year did S'mores Crunch debut?1982
Graham squares, chocolate, and marshmallow — returned in spirit as Smorz in 2003.
When was Reptar Crunch discontinued?1999
A limited Rugrats cereal tied to the first Rugrats movie.
What kind of product was Fruit Stripe?Gum
Tattooed stripes on every stick; the flavor was famously brief.
Which company made The Addams Family Cereal?Ralston
Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
When was SnackWell's Devil's Food Cookie Cakes discontinued?2018
The fat-free era's emblem — the diet cookies people ate by the sleeve.
Which company made McLean Deluxe?McDonald's
The 91%-fat-free burger bound with seaweed extract; the decade's diet-burger monument.
Which came first, Quisp or S'mores Crunch?Quisp — 1965, beating S'mores Crunch (1982) by 17 year(s).
The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
OK Soda came from Coca-Cola. Which other product did Coca-Cola give the decade?New Coke
The 79-day reformulation fiasco that made Coca-Cola Classic a comeback story.
Burger King Kids Club came from Burger King. Name another Burger King product.The Whopper
The flame-grilled standard since 1957.
Which came first, Nintendo Cereal System or Necco Wafers?Necco Wafers — 1847, beating Nintendo Cereal System (1988) by 141 year(s).
The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
Which came first, Burger King Kids Club or Sierra Mist?Burger King Kids Club — 1990, beating Sierra Mist (1999) by 9 year(s).
The Kids Club gang — Kid Vid, Boomer, I.Q., Jaws, and Snaps — on every box and cup.
Which came first, Fruitopia or Ghostbusters Cereal?Ghostbusters Cereal — 1985, beating Fruitopia (1994) by 9 year(s).
Ghost-shaped marshmallows and Slimer on the box.
