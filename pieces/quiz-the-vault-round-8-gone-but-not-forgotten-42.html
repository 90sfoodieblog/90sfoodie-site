The Vault, Round 8: Gone But Not Forgotten

The The Vault, Round 8: Gone But Not Forgotten round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In The Vault, Round 8: Gone But Not Forgotten the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

  1. Which company gave us Batman Cereal?
    A. Wrigley
    B. Jolt
    C. Chipwich
    D. Ralston
  2. What was the famous line attached to Crystal Pepsi?
    A. You've never seen a taste like this
    B. Do the Dew
    C. Made from the best stuff on Earth
    D. Gotta have my Pops

  6. Crystal Pepsi came from Pepsi. Which of these did too?
    A. Cry Baby

  10. Which debuted first — Doritos 3D's, Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee, Surge, or KFC Chicken Littles?
    A. Doritos 3D's
    B. Surge
    C. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee
    D. KFC Chicken Littles

The Answer Key

  1. D. Batman Cereal — Ralston, 1989–1990. Bat-shaped cereal for Tim Burton's summer.
  2. A. Crystal Pepsi — Pepsi, 1992–1994. The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
  3. D. Froot Loops — Kellogg's, 1963. Toucan Sam followed his nose; the loops were all one flavor despite the colors.
  4. A. King Vitaman — Quaker, 1970–2019. The crowned king of the value shelf, poured for nearly fifty years.
  5. A. Chips Ahoy! — Nabisco, 1963. The blue bag of the cookie aisle.
  6. C. Pepsi A.M. — Pepsi, 1989–1990. A higher-caffeine 'morning cola,' gone within a year.
  7. D. Keebler Magic Middles — Keebler, 1988–1995. Shortbread cookies with fudge middles — filed here by vending-machine logic.
  8. B. Necco Wafers — Necco, 1847–2018. The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
  9. A. Squeezit — Betty Crocker, 1985–2001. Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
  10. C. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
