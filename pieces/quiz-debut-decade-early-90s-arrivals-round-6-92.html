The Answer Key

  1. C. Flintstones Push-Up — Nestlé, 1990. Sherbet push tubes with Bedrock's finest on the wrapper.
  2. C. Rice Krispies Treats Cereal — Kellogg's, 1993. The marshmallow treat reimagined as a spoonable cereal.
  3. D. Sun Chips — Frito-Lay, 1991. Multigrain wavy chips; Harvest Cheddar defined the 'better chip' era.
  4. C. Ouch! Bubble Gum — Ouch!, 1990. Bandage-shaped gum in a tin that looked like a first-aid kit.
  5. B. Reese's Puffs — General Mills, 1994. Peanut butter and cocoa puffs — the candy-bar cereal of 1994.
  6. C. Ouch! Bubble Gum — Ouch!, 1990. Bandage-shaped gum in a tin that looked like a first-aid kit.
  7. D. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  8. A. McLean Deluxe — McDonald's, 1991–1996. The 91%-fat-free burger bound with seaweed extract; the decade's diet-burger monument.
  9. D. Kid Cuisine — Kid Cuisine, 1990. The kids' TV dinner with the penguin, a brownie, and corn in the same tray universe.
  10. A. The Addams Family Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1992. Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
