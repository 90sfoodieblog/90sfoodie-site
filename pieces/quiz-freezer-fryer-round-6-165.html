The Answer Key

  1. A. McDonald's Happy Meal — McDonald's, 1979. The boxed meal with a toy — the toy became the point by the 90s.
  2. B. Crazy Bread — Little Caesars, 1982. Butter-and-parmesan breadsticks with Crazy Sauce.
  3. B. Subway — Subway, 1965. Founded as Pete's Super Submarines in 1965; the 90s made it the 'fresh' alternative.
  4. A. Klondike Bar — Klondike, 1922. The jingle's question ran all decade.
  5. C. Toaster Strudel — Pillsbury, 1985. Icing packets you applied yourself — the toaster pastry arms race of 1985.
  6. D. Superman Ice Cream — Superman Ice Cream, early years. The red-yellow-blue Midwest scoop-shop tricolor.
  7. D. Bigfoot Pizza — Pizza Hut, 1993–1995. Two square feet of pizza for $10.99, delivered in a giant box.
  8. B. Popsicle — Popsicle, 1905. Invented by an 11-year-old who left a soda out in the cold; twin pops split for sharing.
  9. B. McLean Deluxe — McDonald's, 1991–1996. The 91%-fat-free burger bound with seaweed extract; the decade's diet-burger monument.
