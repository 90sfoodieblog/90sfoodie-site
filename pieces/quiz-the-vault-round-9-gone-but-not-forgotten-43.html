The Vault, Round 9: Gone But Not Forgotten

Ten questions on The Vault, Round 9: Gone But Not Forgotten — every one drawn from the archive's own record, every answer checked against the shelf it came from. The The Vault, Round 9: Gone But Not Forgotten key waits at the bottom; no peeking, that's the rule of the rewind.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Keebler Magic Middles — Keebler, 1988–1995. Shortbread cookies with fudge middles — filed here by vending-machine logic.
  2. C. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
  3. D. Bill & Ted's Excellent Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1991. A most excellent tie-in, gone as fast as the phone booth.
  4. C. Butterfinger BB's — Nestlé, 1992–2006. Marble-sized Butterfingers, fronted by Bart Simpson at the height of Simpsons mania.
  5. A. Reese's Puffs — General Mills, 1994. Peanut butter and cocoa puffs — the candy-bar cereal of 1994.
  6. C. Wendy's SuperBar — Wendy's, 1980–1998. The all-you-can-eat salad, pasta, and taco bar inside Wendy's — a buffet time capsule.
  7. D. Josta — Pepsi, 1995–1999. Pepsi's guarana soda — the first energy drink from a major soda company.
  8. C. Pepsi A.M. — Pepsi, 1989–1990. A higher-caffeine 'morning cola,' gone within a year.
  9. C. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  10. D. Fruitopia Fruit Integration — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Fruitopia's flagship flavor name, peak 90s wordplay in a bottle.
