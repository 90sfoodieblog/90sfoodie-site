The Answer Key

  1. D. Taco Bell Chihuahua — Taco Bell, 1997–2000. Gidget the chihuahua was 1997's most quoted dog; she also wanted Godzilla tickets.
  2. A. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
  3. C. Taco Bell Value Menu — Taco Bell, 1988. The 59-79-99 era made Taco Bell the broke teenager's banquet.
  4. A. KFC Chicken Littles — KFC, 1986–1990. Tiny chicken sandwiches for pocket change, gone too soon, revived decades later as Littles.
  5. B. McRib — McDonald's, 1981. The rib-shaped pork sandwich whose farewell tours never end.
  6. D. Big Mac — McDonald's, 1968. The jingle lists the whole build; a Pittsburgh creation that went national in 1968.
  7. D. Arby's — Arby's, 1964. The name is R.B. — Raffel Brothers, and roast beef — not America spelled out.
  8. C. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
  9. B. Chicken McNuggets — McDonald's, 1983. Debuted nationally in 1983; the sauces were half the ritual.
  10. D. Popcorn Chicken — KFC, 1992. Bite-size fried chicken in a bucket-shaped cup, a test item that never left.
