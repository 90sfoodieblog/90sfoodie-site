The Answer Key

  1. C. Reptar Crunch — Post, 1999–1999. A limited Rugrats cereal tied to the first Rugrats movie.
  2. C. Stuffed Crust Pizza — Pizza Hut, 1995. Cheese sealed in the crust — eat the pizza backwards, said the ads.
  3. B. Uncrustables — Smucker's, 1995. Sealed crustless PB&J, patented and freezer-born — the lunchbox shortcut of 1995.
  4. A. M&M's Minis — Mars, 1996. Tiny M&M's in a flip-top tube — a pocket status symbol.
  5. B. Uncrustables — Smucker's, 1995. Sealed crustless PB&J, patented and freezer-born — the lunchbox shortcut of 1995.
  6. D. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  7. A. Scooby-Doo Fruit Snacks — Betty Crocker, 1999. The opaque blue Scooby gummy was a flavor mystery of its own.
  8. C. SoBe — SoBe, 1996. South Beach teas and elixirs with the dual-lizard logo.
  9. A. Uncrustables — Smucker's, 1995. Sealed crustless PB&J, patented and freezer-born — the lunchbox shortcut of 1995.
