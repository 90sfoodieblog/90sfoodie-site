The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 5

This is The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 5 — a ten-question round built entirely from documented shelf history. The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 5 asks no trick questions and repeats no rumors: the archive only asks what it can prove. The The Vault Deep Cuts, Round 5 key is printed at the end, the archive way.

The Answer Key

  1. D. Sprinkle Spangles — General Mills, 1993–1998. A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
  2. D. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
  3. D. Oreo O's — Post, 1998–2007. Chocolate rings with marshmallow bits; left the US in 2007, returned a decade later.
  4. B. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
  5. D. TMNT Pudding Pies — Hostess, 1990–1992. Hostess's vanilla pudding pies with green 'mutant' glaze for the Ninja Turtles craze.
  6. A. Urkel-O's — Ralston, 1991–1992. A Family Matters cereal — did it do that? It did, briefly.
  7. A. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  8. D. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
  9. D. McDonald's Fried Apple Pie — McDonald's, 1968–1992. The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
  10. B. Ecto Cooler — Hi-C, 1987–2001. The green tangerine juice box with Slimer on the front, outliving the cartoon by a decade.
