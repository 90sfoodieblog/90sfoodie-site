The Answer Key

  1. A. Hot Tamales — Just Born, 1950. Cinnamon heat in a red capsule — Mike and Ike's spicy shadow.
  2. B. Mary Jane — Mary Jane, 1914. Peanut butter and molasses chews in the yellow wrapper.
  3. C. Boston Market — Boston Market, 1985. Boston Chicken's rotisserie boom renamed the chain Boston Market in 1995.
  4. C. Cooler Ranch Doritos — Frito-Lay, 1986. Launched as Cooler Ranch in 1986; renamed Cool Ranch for international shelves.
  5. C. Zebra Cakes — Little Debbie, early years. Striped white-and-chocolate snack cakes.
  6. A. Mondo — Jel Sert, 1991. Squeezable fruit drinks in the Mondo bottle, a lunchbox currency.
  7. A. Bazooka — Topps, 1947. The gum wrapped in a tiny comic about Bazooka Joe and his eyepatch.
  8. B. Choco Taco — Klondike, 1983–2022. The taco-shaped ice cream, mourned nationwide when Klondike retired it in 2022.
  9. D. Orbitz — Clearly Canadian, 1997–1998. The lava-lamp drink: flavored water with suspended gel balls floating in it.
  10. D. McDLT — McDonald's, 1984–1990. Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
