The Vault, Round 10: Gone But Not Forgotten

The Vault, Round 10: Gone But Not Forgotten: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if The Vault, Round 10: Gone But Not Forgotten asks it, the file answered it first. In The Vault, Round 10: Gone But Not Forgotten, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

The Answer Key

  1. A. Josta — Pepsi, 1995–1999. Pepsi's guarana soda — the first energy drink from a major soda company.
  2. A. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  3. A. Choco Taco — Klondike, 1983–2022. The taco-shaped ice cream, mourned nationwide when Klondike retired it in 2022.
  4. D. Vienna Fingers — Keebler, early years. The oval vanilla sandwich cookie of the office tray.
  5. C. Fruitopia — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Kaleidoscope-colored fruit drinks with names like Strawberry Passion Awareness.
  6. B. Crystal Pepsi — Pepsi, 1992–1994. The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
  7. C. Oreo O's — Post, 1998–2007. Chocolate rings with marshmallow bits; left the US in 2007, returned a decade later.
  8. D. KFC Chicken Littles — KFC, 1986–1990. Tiny chicken sandwiches for pocket change, gone too soon, revived decades later as Littles.
  9. C. Fruity Yummy Mummy — General Mills, 1987–1992. The fifth monster cereal, gone before the decade turned.
  10. B. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
