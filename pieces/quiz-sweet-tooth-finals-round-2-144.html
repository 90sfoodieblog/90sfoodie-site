Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 2

The Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 2 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 2 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

    B. Tootsie
    C. General Foods
    D. Hi-C

    B. 1924
    C. 1944
    D. 1929

The Answer Key

  1. B. Dots — Tootsie, 1945. Gumdrops in the yellow box, straight from the theater counter.
  2. B. Dum Dums — Spangler, 1924. The mystery flavor is one flavor's run crossing into the next.
  3. B. Fruit Stripe — Fruit Stripe, 1960–2024. Tattooed stripes on every stick; the flavor was famously brief.
  4. D. Nik-L-Nip — Nik-L-Nip, early years. Wax bottles you bit, sipped, and chewed — the candy that was also a toy.
  5. D. Hubba Bubba — Wrigley, 1979. Bigger bubbles, better burps in the playground rankings.
  6. B. Trolli Gummi Worms — Trolli, 1981. Gummi worms crawled out of Germany in 1981 and into every 90s candy bin.
  7. D. Peanut Butter M&M's — Mars, 1989. Peanut butter joined the shell game at the decade's edge.
  8. B. Crispy M&M's — Mars, 1999–2005. The orange-shelled crispy M&M, gone from the US in 2005 and revived a decade later.
  9. C. Mary Jane — Mary Jane, 1914. Peanut butter and molasses chews in the yellow wrapper.
  10. C. Skittles — Mars, 1982. The rainbow's US arrival; Wild Berry joined in 1989.
