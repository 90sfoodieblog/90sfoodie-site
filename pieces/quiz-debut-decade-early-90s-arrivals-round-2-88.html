The Answer Key

  1. A. Fruitopia Fruit Integration — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Fruitopia's flagship flavor name, peak 90s wordplay in a bottle.
  2. B. Reese's Puffs — General Mills, 1994. Peanut butter and cocoa puffs — the candy-bar cereal of 1994.
  3. D. Buncha Crunch — Nestlé, 1994. Bunched Crunch pieces for the movies, launched in 1994.
  4. D. Bill & Ted's Excellent Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1991. A most excellent tie-in, gone as fast as the phone booth.
  5. B. Hardee's Frisco Burger — Hardee's, 1992. The sourdough melt that headlined Hardee's early-90s premium run.
  6. B. Kid Cuisine — Kid Cuisine, 1990. The kids' TV dinner with the penguin, a brownie, and corn in the same tray universe.
  7. C. SnackWell's Devil's Food Cookie Cakes — Nabisco, 1992–2018. The fat-free era's emblem — the diet cookies people ate by the sleeve.
  8. D. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  9. D. Buncha Crunch — Nestlé, 1994. Bunched Crunch pieces for the movies, launched in 1994.
  10. A. Bubble Jug — Trolli, 1990. Powder that turned to gum in your mouth, shaken from a jug.
