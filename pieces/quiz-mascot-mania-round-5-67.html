Mascot Mania, Round 5

Ten questions on Mascot Mania, Round 5 — every one drawn from the archive's own record, every answer checked against the shelf it came from. The Mascot Mania, Round 5 key waits at the bottom; no peeking, that's the rule of the rewind.

  1. Bazooka came from Topps. Which of these did too?
    A. Creamsicle
    B. Ring Pop
    C. Burger Bundles
    D. Golden Crisp
  2. Who fronted Golden Crisp in the ads?
    A. Kid Vid
    B. King Ding Dong
    C. Cheesasaurus Rex
    D. Sugar Bear

    A. Fritos

    A. Lucky Charms
    B. Oatmeal Creme Pies
    C. Otter Pops
    D. Hawaiian Punch

The Answer Key

  1. B. Ring Pop — Topps, 1979. The jewel you wore and ate, invented by a dad for his daughter's thumb habit.
  2. D. Golden Crisp — Post, 1949. Sugar Bear crooned it, cool as a jazz singer.
  3. C. Tato Skins — Keebler, 1985. Potato-skin chips in cheddar and bacon, from the hollow tree.
  4. D. Famous Amos Cookies — Famous Amos, 1975. Bite-size chocolate chips from a talent agent turned baker.
  5. C. Andy Capp's Hot Fries — Andy Capp's, 1971. Fry-shaped corn snacks with a comic-strip namesake.
  6. C. Chocodiles — Hostess, early years. The chocolate-covered Twinkie, hunted for decades by its mascot Chauncey.
  7. C. Fruit Stripe — Fruit Stripe, 1960–2024. Tattooed stripes on every stick; the flavor was famously brief.
  8. A. Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries — Quaker, 1967. The berry pieces turned the milk pink-purple.
  9. C. Frosted Flakes — Kellogg's, 1952. Tony's been roaring since 1952.
  10. D. Hawaiian Punch — Hawaiian Punch, 1934. Punchy offered a punch, and took one, in every spot.
