The Answer Key

  1. B. Chick-fil-A Chicken Sandwich — Chick-fil-A, 1964. Pressure-cooked with pickle chips; the mall food court's southern anchor.
  2. A. Dove Bar — Dove, 1984. Thick chocolate over vanilla — the premium bar of the freezer case.
  3. C. Bagel Bites — Bagel Bites, 1985. When pizza's on a bagel, you can eat pizza any time — the jingle is the record.
  4. C. Dippin' Dots — Dippin' Dots, 1988. Flash-frozen beads invented by a microbiologist in 1988; the future lasted decades at malls and ballparks.
  5. A. Subway — Subway, 1965. Founded as Pete's Super Submarines in 1965; the 90s made it the 'fresh' alternative.
  6. C. Crazy Bread — Little Caesars, 1982. Butter-and-parmesan breadsticks with Crazy Sauce.
  7. B. Ben & Jerry's — Ben & Jerry's, 1978. Vermont's finest; Cherry Garcia (1987) and Phish Food (1997) were the decade's pints.
  8. A. Chick-fil-A Chicken Sandwich — Chick-fil-A, 1964. Pressure-cooked with pickle chips; the mall food court's southern anchor.
  9. B. TCBY — TCBY, 1981. The frozen-yogurt era's flagship — soft serve swirls in every mall.
  10. D. McDLT — McDonald's, 1984–1990. Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
