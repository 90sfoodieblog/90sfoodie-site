Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 5

Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 5 deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in Legends of the Pre-90s Shelf, Round 5, the record does the talking.

    A. Fla-Vor-Ice
    B. McDonald's
    C. Pepperidge Farm
    D. Necco

The Answer Key

  1. B. McDonald's Happy Meal — McDonald's, 1979. The boxed meal with a toy — the toy became the point by the 90s.
  2. A. Ruffles — Frito-Lay, 1947. Ridges engineered for dips — 'Ruffles have ridges.'
  3. C. Cocoa Pebbles — Post, 1971. The chocolate twin of the Pebbles line.
  4. C. Junior Mints — Tootsie, 1949. The movie-theater mint, Seinfeld's operating-room candy in 1993.
  5. C. Drumstick — Nestlé, 1928. The cone with the chocolate nugget at the bottom — the reward for finishing.
  6. D. Ruffles — Frito-Lay, 1947. Ridges engineered for dips — 'Ruffles have ridges.'
  7. A. Totino's Pizza Rolls — Totino's, 1968. The molten pocket squares — patience was the only safe temperature.
  8. C. Corn Nuts — Corn Nuts, 1936. Toasted corn kernels, ranch and original, from the gas-station rack.
  9. D. Charms Blow Pop — Charms, 1973. A lollipop with gum at the center — two candies, one stick.
  10. A. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
