The Answer Key

  1. C. Bigfoot Pizza — Pizza Hut, 1993–1995. Two square feet of pizza for $10.99, delivered in a giant box.
  2. C. Arby's — Arby's, 1964. The name is R.B. — Raffel Brothers, and roast beef — not America spelled out.
  3. A. Taco Bell Chihuahua — Taco Bell, 1997–2000. Gidget the chihuahua was 1997's most quoted dog; she also wanted Godzilla tickets.
  4. C. Sonic Drive-In — Sonic, 1953. Carhops on skates and a happy hour of half-price drinks.
  5. A. Snack Pack Pudding — Hunt's, 1968. The pudding cup of Billy Madison fame; the tab-top lid was a hazard and a rite.
  6. B. Taco Bell Value Menu — Taco Bell, 1988. The 59-79-99 era made Taco Bell the broke teenager's banquet.
  7. D. Chuck E. Cheese — Chuck E. Cheese, 1977. Founded by Atari's Nolan Bushnell; absorbed ShowBiz Pizza in the 90s and kept the mouse.
  8. C. Shark Bites — Betty Crocker, 1988. Shark-shaped fruit snacks; the great white was the rare prize.
  9. D. Chuck E. Cheese — Chuck E. Cheese, 1977. Founded by Atari's Nolan Bushnell; absorbed ShowBiz Pizza in the 90s and kept the mouse.
  10. A. McPizza — McDonald's, 1989–2000. McDonald's pizza slowed the drive-thru to a crawl; gone by 2000 in most stores.
