The Answer Key

  1. B. Munchos — Frito-Lay, 1971. The crispy potato snack with the deep-voiced Muppet-ish pitchman.
  2. D. Corn Pops — Kellogg's, 1950. Called Sugar Corn Pops until 1984's name-shortening era.
  3. C. Pringles — Kellogg's, 1968. Chips engineered as identical saddles, stacked in a can to never break. The slogan debuted in 1994.
  4. A. Chef Boyardee Beef Ravioli — Chef Boyardee, 1928. The real chef was Hector Boiardi of Cleveland; the can spelled it phonetically.
  5. C. Klondike Bar — Klondike, 1922. The jingle's question ran all decade.
  6. C. Chocodiles — Hostess, early years. The chocolate-covered Twinkie, hunted for decades by its mascot Chauncey.
  7. C. Dairy Queen Blizzard — Dairy Queen, 1985. So thick it was served upside down — the flip was the proof.
  8. A. Hydrox — Hydrox, 1908–2015. The original chocolate sandwich cookie — Oreo was the copycat, in 1912.
  9. D. Ding Dongs — Hostess, 1967. A chocolate hockey puck of cake and cream.
  10. A. Oatmeal Creme Pies — Little Debbie, 1960. Little Debbie's original — two soft oatmeal cookies around creme.
