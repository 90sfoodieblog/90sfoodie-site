The Answer Key

  1. A. King Vitaman — Quaker, 1970–2019. The crowned king of the value shelf, poured for nearly fifty years.
  2. A. S'mores Crunch — General Mills, 1982–1988. Graham squares, chocolate, and marshmallow — returned in spirit as Smorz in 2003.
  3. C. Taco Bell Chihuahua — Taco Bell, 1997–2000. Gidget the chihuahua was 1997's most quoted dog; she also wanted Godzilla tickets.
  4. C. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  5. B. Sno Balls — Hostess, 1947. Pink marshmallow snowballs with coconut — the color was the flavor's whole identity.
  6. D. Lucky Charms — General Mills, 1964. The marshmallow count kept growing through the 90s — blue diamonds arrived in 1998.
  7. D. The Noid — Domino's, 1986–1995. The claymation villain who ruined pizzas — Domino's mascot of chaos.
  8. C. Squeezit — Betty Crocker, 1985–2001. Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
  9. D. Necco Wafers — Necco, 1847–2018. The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
  10. A. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
