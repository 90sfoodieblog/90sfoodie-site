The Drink Tank, Round 7

The Drink Tank, Round 7: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if The Drink Tank, Round 7 asks it, the file answered it first. In The Drink Tank, Round 7, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

The Answer Key

  1. D. Arizona Iced Tea — Arizona, 1992. The tall 99-cent can that never raised its price.
  2. B. Capri Sun — Capri Sun, 1981. The silver pouch and the straw-stab ritual of every lunchbox.
  3. C. Little Hug — Little Hug, 1974. The little barrel drinks sold for pocket change.
  4. D. Gatorade — Pepsico, 1965. The 1991 'Be Like Mike' jingle made sports drink a Michael Jordan anthem.
  5. C. Brisk Iced Tea — Lipton, 1991. Claymation celebrity ads made Brisk a 90s catchphrase machine.
  6. D. Squeezit — Betty Crocker, 1985–2001. Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
  7. C. Barq's Root Beer — Barq's, 1898. The caffeinated root beer with bite, from Biloxi in 1898.
  8. D. Orbitz — Clearly Canadian, 1997–1998. The lava-lamp drink: flavored water with suspended gel balls floating in it.
  9. A. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
  10. B. Ecto Cooler — Hi-C, 1987–2001. The green tangerine juice box with Slimer on the front, outliving the cartoon by a decade.
