Trivia Night: The Breakfast Club, Round 8

Fifteen rapid-fire questions for The Breakfast Club, Round 8, answers face-up underneath — this is the written kind of trivia night, the archive's way. Read the The Breakfast Club, Round 8 questions out loud for full effect.

QuestionAnswer & the story
Fig Newtons came from Nabisco. What else did Nabisco put on the shelves?Animal Crackers (Barnum's)
The circus-cage box with a string handle, a Christmas ornament by design.
Fruity Pebbles came from Post. Name one more from the Post shelf.Reptar Crunch
A limited Rugrats cereal tied to the first Rugrats movie.
Lucky Charms came from General Mills. Besides Lucky Charms, name a General Mills sibling.Hidden Treasures
Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
Which came first, Teddy Grahams or Reese's Puffs?Teddy Grahams — 1988, beating Reese's Puffs (1994) by 6 year(s).
Bear-shaped grahams that danced through 90s commercials in sunglasses.
Which came first, E.L. Fudge or Fig Newtons?Fig Newtons — 1891, beating E.L. Fudge (1986) by 95 year(s).
Named for Newton, Massachusetts — not the physicist.
Ho Hos came from Hostess. Name another Hostess product.Sno Balls
Pink marshmallow snowballs with coconut — the color was the flavor's whole identity.
Cocoa Puffs came from General Mills. What else did General Mills put on the shelves?Honey Nut Cheerios
The honey-nut bowl that outsold the original.
Which came first, Cap'n Crunch or S'mores Crunch?Cap'n Crunch — 1963, beating S'mores Crunch (1982) by 19 year(s).
The Cap'n stayed crunchy, even in milk — the box's eternal promise.
Gushers came from Betty Crocker. Which other product did Betty Crocker give the decade?Fruit by the Foot
Three feet of fruit snack, unrolled like a measuring tape.
Zebra Cakes came from Little Debbie. Name another Little Debbie product.Oatmeal Creme Pies
Little Debbie's original — two soft oatmeal cookies around creme.
Which came first, Mrs. Fields Cookies or Famous Amos Cookies?Famous Amos Cookies — 1975, beating Mrs. Fields Cookies (1977) by 2 year(s).
Bite-size chocolate chips from a talent agent turned baker.
Which came first, Waffle Crisp or Frute Brute?Frute Brute — 1974, beating Waffle Crisp (1996) by 22 year(s).
The werewolf's fruit cereal, retired in the 80s and revived for Halloween seasons.
Reese's Puffs came from General Mills. Name one more from the General Mills shelf.Wheaties
The orange box put Michael Jordan on more breakfast tables than anyone.
Nilla Wafers came from Nabisco. Name another Nabisco product.Fig Newtons
Named for Newton, Massachusetts — not the physicist.
Which came first, TMNT Pudding Pies or Corn Pops?Corn Pops — 1950, beating TMNT Pudding Pies (1990) by 40 year(s).
Called Sugar Corn Pops until 1984's name-shortening era.
