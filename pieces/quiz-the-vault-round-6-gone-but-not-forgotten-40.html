The Answer Key

  1. B. Burger King Kids Club — Burger King, 1990–2000. The Kids Club gang — Kid Vid, Boomer, I.Q., Jaws, and Snaps — on every box and cup.
  2. C. McDLT — McDonald's, 1984–1990. Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
  3. B. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  4. A. Crispy M&M's — Mars, 1999–2005. The orange-shelled crispy M&M, gone from the US in 2005 and revived a decade later.
  5. D. Sprinkle Spangles — General Mills, 1993–1998. A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
  6. C. Nintendo Cereal System — Ralston, 1988–1989. Two cereals in one box — Super Mario fruit and Zelda berry, split down the middle.
  7. A. Crystal Pepsi — Pepsi, 1992–1994. The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
  8. B. Fruitopia Fruit Integration — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Fruitopia's flagship flavor name, peak 90s wordplay in a bottle.
  9. A. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  10. D. The Addams Family Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1992. Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
