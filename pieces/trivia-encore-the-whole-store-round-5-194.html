Trivia Night: Encore: The Whole Store, Round 5

Encore: The Whole Store, Round 5: fifteen questions, fifteen answers with the story underneath each one. Everything on the Encore: The Whole Store, Round 5 card comes from the archive's record — the same facts the reference entries file in full.

QuestionAnswer & the story
Cheez-It came from Kellogg's. Name one more from the Kellogg's shelf.Rice Krispies
The elves' names are the sound of the cereal in milk.
Boo Berry came from General Mills. Besides Boo Berry, name a General Mills sibling.Lucky Charms
The marshmallow count kept growing through the 90s — blue diamonds arrived in 1998.
Which came first, Apple Jacks or Fruit by the Foot?Apple Jacks — 1965, beating Fruit by the Foot (1991) by 26 year(s).
The ads' eternal mystery: they don't taste like apples, and the kids didn't care.
Which came first, Froot Loops or Milk Duds?Milk Duds — 1926, beating Froot Loops (1963) by 37 year(s).
Caramel 'duds' — named for the round shape the machine couldn't make.
Which came first, Golden Grahams or Whatchamacallit?Golden Grahams — 1976, beating Whatchamacallit (1978) by 2 year(s).
Honey graham squares that anchored a decade of s'mores-flavored everything.
Which came first, Arby's or Batman Cereal?Arby's — 1964, beating Batman Cereal (1989) by 25 year(s).
The name is R.B. — Raffel Brothers, and roast beef — not America spelled out.
Which came first, Mondo or Necco Wafers?Necco Wafers — 1847, beating Mondo (1991) by 144 year(s).
The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
Peanut Butter M&M's came from Mars. Which other product did Mars give the decade?Snickers
Named for the Mars family's horse; the 90s bar of choice at every checkout.
Butterfinger BB's came from Nestlé. What else did Nestlé put on the shelves?Runts
Fruit-shaped hard candy — the banana was the prize.
Which came first, Pillsbury Doughboy or Snapple?Pillsbury Doughboy — 1961, beating Snapple (1972) by 11 year(s).
Poppin' Fresh has giggled at a finger poke since 1961.
Which came first, McDonald's Happy Meal or Push Pop?McDonald's Happy Meal — 1979, beating Push Pop (1986) by 7 year(s).
The boxed meal with a toy — the toy became the point by the 90s.
Which came first, Faygo or Pepsi A.M.?Faygo — 1907, beating Pepsi A.M. (1989) by 82 year(s).
Detroit's soda — Rock & Rye — and the Insane Clown Posse's eternal spray.
Which came first, Good & Plenty or Rice Krispies Treats Cereal?Good & Plenty — 1893, beating Rice Krispies Treats Cereal (1993) by 100 year(s).
Licorice pellets in pink and white, shaken like a train by Choo Choo Charlie.
Chicken McNuggets came from McDonald's. Which other product did McDonald's give the decade?McDLT
Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
Trix came from General Mills. Which other product did General Mills give the decade?Bugles
Corn horns worn on fingertips as claws in every classroom.
