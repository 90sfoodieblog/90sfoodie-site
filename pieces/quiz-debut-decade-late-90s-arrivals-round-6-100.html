The Answer Key

  1. C. Bigfoot Pizza — Pizza Hut, 1993–1995. Two square feet of pizza for $10.99, delivered in a giant box.
  2. A. SoBe — SoBe, 1996. South Beach teas and elixirs with the dual-lizard logo.
  3. A. Doritos 3D's — Frito-Lay, 1998–2004. Puffed 3D Doritos in a plastic canister — the decade's chip engineering marvel.
  4. C. Orbitz — Clearly Canadian, 1997–1998. The lava-lamp drink: flavored water with suspended gel balls floating in it.
  5. A. Jack in the Box Antenna Balls — Jack in the Box, 1995. Jack's comeback era (from 1994) put his head on a million antennas.
  6. B. Reptar Crunch — Post, 1999–1999. A limited Rugrats cereal tied to the first Rugrats movie.
  7. A. Scooby-Doo Fruit Snacks — Betty Crocker, 1999. The opaque blue Scooby gummy was a flavor mystery of its own.
  8. C. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
  9. B. Jack in the Box Antenna Balls — Jack in the Box, 1995. Jack's comeback era (from 1994) put his head on a million antennas.
  10. D. Orbitz — Clearly Canadian, 1997–1998. The lava-lamp drink: flavored water with suspended gel balls floating in it.
