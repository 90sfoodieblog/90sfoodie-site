Trivia Night: The Breakfast Club, Round 4

This is the The Breakfast Club, Round 4 card: fifteen rapid-fire Q&As drawn from the documented shelf. No apps, no timers in The Breakfast Club, Round 4 — just the record, dealt face-up, the way the archive plays it.

QuestionAnswer & the story
Which came first, Apple Jacks or King Vitaman?Apple Jacks — 1965, beating King Vitaman (1970) by 5 year(s).
The ads' eternal mystery: they don't taste like apples, and the kids didn't care.
Which came first, Morning Funnies or Honey Smacks?Honey Smacks — 1953, beating Morning Funnies (1988) by 35 year(s).
Dig 'Em hopped onto the box in 1972; the cereal was Sugar Smacks before 1984.
Which came first, Twinkies or Honey Smacks?Twinkies — 1930, beating Honey Smacks (1953) by 23 year(s).
The golden snack cake that survived even Hostess's 2012 shutdown, returning in 2013.
Which came first, Frosted Flakes or Bill & Ted's Excellent Cereal?Frosted Flakes — 1952, beating Bill & Ted's Excellent Cereal (1991) by 39 year(s).
Tony's been roaring since 1952.
Reptar Crunch came from Post. What else did Post put on the shelves?Cocoa Pebbles
The chocolate twin of the Pebbles line.
Which came first, Trix or Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries?Trix — 1954, beating Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries (1967) by 13 year(s).
Turned fruit-shaped in 1991, the decade's defining cereal-box silhouette.
Which came first, Sprinkle Spangles or French Toast Crunch?Sprinkle Spangles — 1993, beating French Toast Crunch (1995) by 2 year(s).
A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
Which came first, Nilla Wafers or Famous Amos Cookies?Nilla Wafers — 1898, beating Famous Amos Cookies (1975) by 77 year(s).
The vanilla wafer that banana pudding cannot live without.
Which came first, Morning Funnies or S'mores Crunch?S'mores Crunch — 1982, beating Morning Funnies (1988) by 6 year(s).
Graham squares, chocolate, and marshmallow — returned in spirit as Smorz in 2003.
Which came first, Frute Brute or Oatmeal Creme Pies?Oatmeal Creme Pies — 1960, beating Frute Brute (1974) by 14 year(s).
Little Debbie's original — two soft oatmeal cookies around creme.
Which came first, Hidden Treasures or TMNT Pudding Pies?TMNT Pudding Pies — 1990, beating Hidden Treasures (1993) by 3 year(s).
Hostess's vanilla pudding pies with green 'mutant' glaze for the Ninja Turtles craze.
Which came first, Mallomars or Mrs. Fields Cookies?Mallomars — 1913, beating Mrs. Fields Cookies (1977) by 64 year(s).
Chocolate marshmallow cookies sold only in cool months, so the chocolate wouldn't melt.
Which came first, Tastykake Butterscotch Krimpets or Batman Cereal?Tastykake Butterscotch Krimpets — 1914, beating Batman Cereal (1989) by 75 year(s).
Philadelphia's sponge cakes with butterscotch icing, from 1914.
Which came first, Pillsbury Doughboy or Cinnamon Toast Crunch?Pillsbury Doughboy — 1961, beating Cinnamon Toast Crunch (1984) by 23 year(s).
Poppin' Fresh has giggled at a finger poke since 1961.
Which came first, Boo Berry or Peanut Butter Crunch?Peanut Butter Crunch — 1969, beating Boo Berry (1973) by 4 year(s).
The peanut butter entry in the Cap'n's fleet.
