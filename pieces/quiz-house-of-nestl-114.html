House of Nestlé

The House of Nestlé round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In House of Nestlé the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Flintstones Push-Up — Nestlé, 1990. Sherbet push tubes with Bedrock's finest on the wrapper.
  2. A. Flintstones Push-Up — Nestlé, 1990. Sherbet push tubes with Bedrock's finest on the wrapper.
  3. C. Nerds — Nestlé, 1983. Tiny tangy pebbles in a two-chamber box, two flavors at war.
  4. A. Drumstick — Nestlé, 1928. The cone with the chocolate nugget at the bottom — the reward for finishing.
  5. B. Laffy Taffy — Nestlé, 1984. Taffy with a joke printed on every wrapper.
  6. D. Laffy Taffy — Nestlé, 1984. Taffy with a joke printed on every wrapper.
  7. B. Pixy Stix — Nestlé, 1952. Powdered sugar in a paper straw — pure sugar, poured.
  8. A. Snowcaps — Nestlé, 1948. Dark chocolate drops with white sprinkles — pure movie candy.
  9. C. Raisinets — Nestlé, 1927. Chocolate-covered raisins, the theater's 'healthy' alibi.
  10. C. Butterfinger BB's — Nestlé, 1992–2006. Marble-sized Butterfingers, fronted by Bart Simpson at the height of Simpsons mania.
