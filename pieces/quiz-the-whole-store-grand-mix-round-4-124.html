The Answer Key

  1. B. Hot Tamales — Just Born, 1950. Cinnamon heat in a red capsule — Mike and Ike's spicy shadow.
  2. B. McLean Deluxe — McDonald's, 1991–1996. The 91%-fat-free burger bound with seaweed extract; the decade's diet-burger monument.
  3. A. McLean Deluxe — McDonald's, 1991–1996. The 91%-fat-free burger bound with seaweed extract; the decade's diet-burger monument.
  4. A. Drumstick — Nestlé, 1928. The cone with the chocolate nugget at the bottom — the reward for finishing.
  5. B. Hawaiian Punch — Hawaiian Punch, 1934. Punchy offered a punch, and took one, in every spot.
  6. D. Creamsicle — Popsicle, 1920. Orange shell, vanilla heart — the twin pop's creamy sibling.
  7. C. Count Chocula — General Mills, 1971. The chocolate monster cereal, a Halloween-season staple.
  8. B. Klondike Bar — Klondike, 1922. The jingle's question ran all decade.
  9. A. Mike and Ike — Just Born, 1940. The fruit-chew duo in the long box.
  10. D. Goldfish — Pepperidge Farm, 1962. The smiling fish crackers got a mascot and a jingle in the 90s.
