Sodas & Drinks: The Rewind

This is Sodas & Drinks: The Rewind — a ten-question round built entirely from documented shelf history. Sodas & Drinks: The Rewind asks no trick questions and repeats no rumors: the archive only asks what it can prove. The Sodas & Drinks: The Rewind key is printed at the end, the archive way.

The Answer Key

  1. B. Powerade — Coca-Cola, 1988. Coke's sports drink, launched into Gatorade's decade.
  2. C. Hawaiian Punch — Hawaiian Punch, 1934. Punchy offered a punch, and took one, in every spot.
  3. B. Sierra Mist — Pepsi, 1999–2023. Pepsi's lemon-lime launch at the decade's edge.
  4. D. Kool-Aid Bursts — Kraft, 1994. Twist-top bottles of Kool-Aid for the lunchbox run, from 1994.
  5. D. Brisk Iced Tea — Lipton, 1991. Claymation celebrity ads made Brisk a 90s catchphrase machine.
  6. C. Pepsi Wild Cherry — Pepsi, 1988. Tested as Wild Cherry Pepsi from 1988; the cherry cola wars' survivor.
  7. D. SoBe — SoBe, 1996. South Beach teas and elixirs with the dual-lizard logo.
  8. D. Orbitz — Clearly Canadian, 1997–1998. The lava-lamp drink: flavored water with suspended gel balls floating in it.
  9. A. Bug Juice — Bug Juice, 1990. The summer-camp canteen staple in the bug-logo bottle.
  10. C. Ecto Cooler — Hi-C, 1987–2001. The green tangerine juice box with Slimer on the front, outliving the cartoon by a decade.
