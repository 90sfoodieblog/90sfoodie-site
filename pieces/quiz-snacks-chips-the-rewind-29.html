Snacks & Chips: The Rewind

Snacks & Chips: The Rewind deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in Snacks & Chips: The Rewind, the record does the talking.

The Answer Key

  1. A. Corn Nuts — Corn Nuts, 1936. Toasted corn kernels, ranch and original, from the gas-station rack.
  2. D. Andy Capp's Hot Fries — Andy Capp's, 1971. Fry-shaped corn snacks with a comic-strip namesake.
  3. A. Andy Capp's Hot Fries — Andy Capp's, 1971. Fry-shaped corn snacks with a comic-strip namesake.
  4. C. Pringles — Kellogg's, 1968. Chips engineered as identical saddles, stacked in a can to never break. The slogan debuted in 1994.
  5. B. Doritos 3D's — Frito-Lay, 1998–2004. Puffed 3D Doritos in a plastic canister — the decade's chip engineering marvel.
  6. A. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  7. D. Tato Skins — Keebler, 1985. Potato-skin chips in cheddar and bacon, from the hollow tree.
  8. A. Cracker Jack — Frito-Lay, 1896. Caramel corn and peanuts with a prize in every box since 1912.
  9. B. Pizzarias — Keebler, 1991–2000. Pizza-flavored chips 'made with real pizza' — a Keebler 90s cult classic.
  10. C. Fritos — Frito-Lay, 1932. The corn chip that built Frito-Lay — and the walking taco's base.
