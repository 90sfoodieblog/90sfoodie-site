Encore: The Whole Store, Round 8

Encore: The Whole Store, Round 8 deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in Encore: The Whole Store, Round 8, the record does the talking.

  1. In what year did Gardetto's first reach shelves?
    A. 1985
    B. 1992
    C. 1995
    D. 1989
  2. What was the famous line attached to Sour Patch Kids?
    A. Milk's favorite cookie
    B. First they're sour, then they're sweet
    C. Be like Mike
    D. That's Brisk, baby!

  10. Which debuted first — Bazooka, Animal Crackers (Barnum's), Uncrustables, or Capri Sun?

    B. Animal Crackers (Barnum's)
    C. Bazooka
    D. Capri Sun

The Answer Key

  1. A. Gardetto's — General Mills, 1985. The Milwaukee bakery snack mix — the rye chip was everyone's pick.
  2. B. Sour Patch Kids — Mondelēz, 1985. First sold as Mars Men in the 70s; the sour-then-sweet switch was the hook.
  3. B. Pizzarias — Keebler, 1991–2000. Pizza-flavored chips 'made with real pizza' — a Keebler 90s cult classic.
  4. C. New Coke — Coca-Cola, 1985–1985. The 79-day reformulation fiasco that made Coca-Cola Classic a comeback story.
  5. C. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  6. A. Mallomars — Nabisco, 1913. Chocolate marshmallow cookies sold only in cool months, so the chocolate wouldn't melt.
  7. A. Haribo Goldbears — Haribo, 1922. The original gummi bears, from Bonn in 1922, in US stores by 1982.
  8. B. Mary Jane — Mary Jane, 1914. Peanut butter and molasses chews in the yellow wrapper.
  9. D. Boo Berry — General Mills, 1973. The blueberry ghost cereal that completed the monster trio.
  10. B. Animal Crackers (Barnum's) — Nabisco, 1902. The circus-cage box with a string handle, a Christmas ornament by design.
