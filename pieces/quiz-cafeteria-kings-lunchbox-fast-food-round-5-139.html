The Answer Key

  1. C. SpaghettiOs — Campbell's, 1965. Franco-American's rings in tomato sauce, thermos-transported by millions.
  2. C. Burger King Kids Club — Burger King, 1990–2000. The Kids Club gang — Kid Vid, Boomer, I.Q., Jaws, and Snaps — on every box and cup.
  3. C. SpaghettiOs — Campbell's, 1965. Franco-American's rings in tomato sauce, thermos-transported by millions.
  4. D. Chuck E. Cheese — Chuck E. Cheese, 1977. Founded by Atari's Nolan Bushnell; absorbed ShowBiz Pizza in the 90s and kept the mouse.
  5. B. SpaghettiOs — Campbell's, 1965. Franco-American's rings in tomato sauce, thermos-transported by millions.
  6. D. Dairy Queen Blizzard — Dairy Queen, 1985. So thick it was served upside down — the flip was the proof.
  7. D. Snack Pack Pudding — Hunt's, 1968. The pudding cup of Billy Madison fame; the tab-top lid was a hazard and a rite.
  8. C. Crazy Bread — Little Caesars, 1982. Butter-and-parmesan breadsticks with Crazy Sauce.
  9. D. Wendy's SuperBar — Wendy's, 1980–1998. The all-you-can-eat salad, pasta, and taco bar inside Wendy's — a buffet time capsule.
  10. A. Jack in the Box Antenna Balls — Jack in the Box, 1995. Jack's comeback era (from 1994) put his head on a million antennas.
