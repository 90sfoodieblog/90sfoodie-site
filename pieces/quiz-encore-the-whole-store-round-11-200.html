Encore: The Whole Store, Round 11: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if Encore: The Whole Store, Round 11 asks it, the file answered it first. In Encore: The Whole Store, Round 11, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

The Answer Key

  1. B. Wheaties — General Mills, 1924. The orange box put Michael Jordan on more breakfast tables than anyone.
  2. D. Circus Peanuts — Circus Peanuts, early years. Orange, peanut-shaped, banana-flavored marshmallow — candy's beloved paradox.
  3. D. Gushers — Betty Crocker, 1991. Fruit snacks with a liquid center that burst — heads turned into fruit in the ads. (Filed here by lunchbox logic.)
  4. D. Nilla Wafers — Nabisco, 1898. The vanilla wafer that banana pudding cannot live without.
  5. C. Handi-Snacks — Kraft, 1980. Crackers and a cheese spread with the little red plastic stick. (A lunchbox crossover king.)
  6. B. Peanut Butter M&M's — Mars, 1989. Peanut butter joined the shell game at the decade's edge.
  7. B. Everlasting Gobstopper — Nestlé, 1976. Willy Wonka's jawbreaker that changed colors and flavors as it shrank.
  8. D. Baby Bottle Pop — Topps, 1998. Dip the bottle top in the powder — the late-90s novelty anthem.
  9. B. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
  10. D. Oreo O's — Post, 1998–2007. Chocolate rings with marshmallow bits; left the US in 2007, returned a decade later.
