Slogan Wars, Round 7

Slogan Wars, Round 7 deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in Slogan Wars, Round 7, the record does the talking.

  1. What was the famous line attached to Bagel Bites?
    A. Follow my nose
    B. That's Brisk, baby!
    C. A cookie? Newton said it's a pastry cake.
    D. Pizza in the morning, pizza in the evening, pizza at suppertime
  2. Which product promised: “Made from the best stuff on Earth”?
    A. Sno Balls
    B. Cooler Ranch Doritos
    C. Snapple
    D. Lemonhead
  3. Which aisle of the 90s did Juicy Juice call home?
    A. Snack Cakes & Cookies
    B. Candy
    C. Sodas & Drinks
    D. Snacks & Chips
  4. What was the famous line attached to TCBY?
    A. Avoid the Noid
    B. Fully Loaded
    C. The Country's Best Yogurt
    D. All the sugar and twice the caffeine
  5. In what year did Goldfish first reach shelves?
    A. 1955
    B. 1969
    C. 1962
    D. 1958
  6. Name the product in this memory: “Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.”
    A. Cheetos
    B. Doritos 3D's
    C. Pringles
    D. Tostitos
  7. When did Taco Bell Chihuahua leave the shelves?
    A. 2003
    B. 2000
    C. 2004
    D. 1990
  8. Which debuted first — Golden Crisp, Dippin' Dots, Fruity Pebbles, or Kix?
    A. Kix
    B. Dippin' Dots
    C. Golden Crisp
    D. Fruity Pebbles
  9. Which debuted first — The Noid, SunnyD, Brisk Iced Tea, or Lucky Charms?
    A. The Noid
    B. Lucky Charms
    C. SunnyD
    D. Brisk Iced Tea
  10. Which debuted first — Crystal Pepsi, Big Mac, Cinnamon Toast Crunch, or Sour Patch Kids?
    A. Cinnamon Toast Crunch
    B. Big Mac
    C. Sour Patch Kids
    D. Crystal Pepsi

The Answer Key

  1. D. Bagel Bites — Bagel Bites, 1985. When pizza's on a bagel, you can eat pizza any time — the jingle is the record.
  2. C. Snapple — Snapple, 1972. Bottled teas and juices whose radio ads made Wendy a folk hero.
  3. C. Juicy Juice — Juicy Juice, 1977. The juice box of the nursery-school set.
  4. C. TCBY — TCBY, 1981. The frozen-yogurt era's flagship — soft serve swirls in every mall.
  5. C. Goldfish — Pepperidge Farm, 1962. The smiling fish crackers got a mascot and a jingle in the 90s.
  6. A. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  7. B. Taco Bell Chihuahua — Taco Bell, 1997–2000. Gidget the chihuahua was 1997's most quoted dog; she also wanted Godzilla tickets.
  8. A. Kix — General Mills, 1937. The first puffed corn cereal, puffed in a gun-like machine.
  9. C. SunnyD — Sunny Delight, 1963. The orange drink kids pulled from the fridge while parents reached for 'the purple stuff' (in the famous ad, the sunny one won).
  10. B. Big Mac — McDonald's, 1968. The jingle lists the whole build; a Pittsburgh creation that went national in 1968.
