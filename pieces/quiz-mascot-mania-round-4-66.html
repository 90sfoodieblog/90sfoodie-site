Mascot Mania, Round 4

The Mascot Mania, Round 4 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Mascot Mania, Round 4 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

  1. Which product promised: “It ain't easy being cheesy”?
    A. Wax Lips
    B. Cheetos
    C. Drake's Devil Dogs
    D. Moose Tracks
  2. Which product promised: “Cuckoo for Cocoa Puffs”?
    A. AirHeads
    B. Doritos 3D's
    C. Little Debbie Swiss Rolls
    D. Cocoa Puffs
  3. When did The Noid leave the shelves?
    A. 1991
    B. 2015
    C. 2000
    D. 1995
  4. Which aisle of the 90s did Cookie Crisp call home?
    A. Lunchbox
    B. Frozen & Ice Cream
    C. Snacks & Chips
    D. Cereals
  5. When did Ghostbusters Cereal leave the shelves?
    A. 1992
    B. 1990
    C. 1989
    D. 1996
  6. Which company gave us Cheetos?
    A. Jel Sert
    B. Barq's
    C. Frito-Lay
    D. Arby's
  7. Name the product in this memory: “The honey-nut bowl that outsold the original.”
    A. Nintendo Cereal System
    B. S'mores Crunch
    C. Honey Nut Cheerios
    D. Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries
  8. Which debuted first — Keebler Magic Middles, Cinnamon Toast Crunch, Taco Bell Chihuahua, or Tato Skins?
    A. Cinnamon Toast Crunch
    B. Keebler Magic Middles
    C. Taco Bell Chihuahua
    D. Tato Skins
  9. Which debuted first — Froot Loops, Burger King Kids Club, Cheetos, or Keebler Magic Middles?
    A. Cheetos
    B. Burger King Kids Club
    C. Keebler Magic Middles
    D. Froot Loops
  10. Which debuted first — Doritos, Peanut Butter Crunch, Goldfish, or Crispy M&M's?
    A. Peanut Butter Crunch
    B. Doritos
    C. Goldfish
    D. Crispy M&M's

The Answer Key

  1. B. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  2. D. Cocoa Puffs — General Mills, 1958. Sonny went cuckoo in every 90s commercial break.
  3. D. The Noid — Domino's, 1986–1995. The claymation villain who ruined pizzas — Domino's mascot of chaos.
  4. D. Cookie Crisp — Ralston, 1977. The cereal that promised cookies for breakfast, sold by a wizard, then a burglar.
  5. C. Ghostbusters Cereal — Ralston, 1985–1989. Ghost-shaped marshmallows and Slimer on the box.
  6. C. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  7. C. Honey Nut Cheerios — General Mills, 1979. The honey-nut bowl that outsold the original.
  8. A. Cinnamon Toast Crunch — General Mills, 1984. Cinnamon-sugar squares; Crazy Squares in the modern ads.
  9. A. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  10. C. Goldfish — Pepperidge Farm, 1962. The smiling fish crackers got a mascot and a jingle in the 90s.
