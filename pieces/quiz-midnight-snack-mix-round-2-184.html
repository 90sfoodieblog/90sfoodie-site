The Answer Key

  1. B. Popsicle — Popsicle, 1905. Invented by an 11-year-old who left a soda out in the cold; twin pops split for sharing.
  2. C. Ding Dongs — Hostess, 1967. A chocolate hockey puck of cake and cream.
  3. D. Sour Patch Kids — Mondelēz, 1985. First sold as Mars Men in the 70s; the sour-then-sweet switch was the hook.
  4. B. Doritos — Frito-Lay, 1966. Born at Disneyland's Casa de Fritos; Nacho Cheese arrived in 1972.
  5. B. The Whopper — Burger King, 1957. The flame-grilled standard since 1957.
  6. C. Pixy Stix — Nestlé, 1952. Powdered sugar in a paper straw — pure sugar, poured.
  7. D. Rice Krispies Treats Cereal — Kellogg's, 1993. The marshmallow treat reimagined as a spoonable cereal.
  8. D. Klondike Bar — Klondike, 1922. The jingle's question ran all decade.
  9. D. Drake's Devil Dogs — Drake's, 1926. Two devil's food cakes with cream — the Northeast lunchbox dog.
  10. A. Arch Deluxe — McDonald's, 1996–2000. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.
