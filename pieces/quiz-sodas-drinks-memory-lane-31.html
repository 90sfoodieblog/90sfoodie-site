The Answer Key

  1. A. Josta — Pepsi, 1995–1999. Pepsi's guarana soda — the first energy drink from a major soda company.
  2. D. Mountain Dew — Pepsi, 1948. The extreme-sports era's fuel; the 90s made it a lifestyle.
  3. A. New Coke — Coca-Cola, 1985–1985. The 79-day reformulation fiasco that made Coca-Cola Classic a comeback story.
  4. D. Mistic — Mistic, 1990. Tropical glass-bottle teas and juices from the Snapple era shelf.
  5. C. Kool-Aid — Kraft, 1927. The pitcher who burst through walls; Bursts bottles joined the pouches in the 90s.
  6. C. New Coke — Coca-Cola, 1985–1985. The 79-day reformulation fiasco that made Coca-Cola Classic a comeback story.
  7. C. Gatorade — Pepsico, 1965. The 1991 'Be Like Mike' jingle made sports drink a Michael Jordan anthem.
  8. D. Little Hug — Little Hug, 1974. The little barrel drinks sold for pocket change.
  9. D. Tang — General Foods, 1957. The astronauts' orange drink, a pantry constant.
  10. A. Capri Sun — Capri Sun, 1981. The silver pouch and the straw-stab ritual of every lunchbox.
