Midnight Snack Mix, Round 4

The Midnight Snack Mix, Round 4 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Midnight Snack Mix, Round 4 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

  1. Name the product in this memory: “The flame-grilled standard since 1957.”
    A. The Whopper
    B. Arch Deluxe
    C. McLean Deluxe
    D. Popcorn Chicken
  2. Name the product in this memory: “Peanut butter and molasses chews in the yellow wrapper.”
    A. Snickers
    B. Mary Jane
    C. Whoppers
    D. Now and Later
  3. In what year did Big League Chew first reach shelves?
    A. 1978
    B. 2000
    C. 1980
    D. 1990
  4. Which aisle of the 90s did Candy Necklaces call home?
    A. Snacks & Chips
    B. Cereals
    C. Lunchbox
    D. Candy
  5. Name the product in this memory: “The owl's question outlived the century.”
    A. Hot Tamales
    B. Tootsie Roll
    C. Atomic Fireballs
    D. Warheads
  6. Which aisle of the 90s did Nerds call home?
    A. Candy

    A. Laffy Taffy
    B. Subway

    A. Bug Juice
    B. Nerds
    C. Chuck E. Cheese
    D. McPizza
  10. Which debuted first — Corn Nuts, Nilla Wafers, Snack Pack Pudding, or Skor?
    A. Skor
    B. Corn Nuts
    C. Nilla Wafers
    D. Snack Pack Pudding

The Answer Key

  1. A. The Whopper — Burger King, 1957. The flame-grilled standard since 1957.
  2. B. Mary Jane — Mary Jane, 1914. Peanut butter and molasses chews in the yellow wrapper.
  3. C. Big League Chew — Big League Chew, 1980. Shredded gum in a pouch, invented in a minor-league bullpen.
  4. D. Candy Necklaces — Candy Necklaces, early years. Wearable sugar beads, chalky and pastel, eaten off the string.
  5. B. Tootsie Roll — Tootsie, 1896. The owl's question outlived the century.
  6. A. Nerds — Nestlé, 1983. Tiny tangy pebbles in a two-chamber box, two flavors at war.
  7. D. Kool-Aid Bursts — Kraft, 1994. Twist-top bottles of Kool-Aid for the lunchbox run, from 1994.
  8. C. Kit Kat — Hershey, 1935. The break-apart bar with the jingle everyone can finish.
  9. D. McPizza — McDonald's, 1989–2000. McDonald's pizza slowed the drive-thru to a crawl; gone by 2000 in most stores.
  10. C. Nilla Wafers — Nabisco, 1898. The vanilla wafer that banana pudding cannot live without.
