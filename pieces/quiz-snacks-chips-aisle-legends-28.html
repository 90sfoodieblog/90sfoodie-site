The Answer Key

  1. B. Cheez-It — Kellogg's, 1921. The square with the hole, baked since Dayton in 1921.
  2. D. Chex Mix — General Mills, 1960. The party mix commercialized; Bold Party Blend turned up the seasoning in the 90s.
  3. C. Combos — Mars, 1978. Cheese-filled pretzel and cracker tubes — road-trip fuel.
  4. C. Tostitos — Frito-Lay, 1979. Restaurant-style tortilla chips; Scoops! turned them into edible spoons in the 90s.
  5. C. Keebler Magic Middles — Keebler, 1988–1995. Shortbread cookies with fudge middles — filed here by vending-machine logic.
  6. A. Bugles — General Mills, 1964. Corn horns worn on fingertips as claws in every classroom.
  7. B. Goldfish — Pepperidge Farm, 1962. The smiling fish crackers got a mascot and a jingle in the 90s.
  8. A. Ruffles — Frito-Lay, 1947. Ridges engineered for dips — 'Ruffles have ridges.'
  9. C. Tato Skins — Keebler, 1985. Potato-skin chips in cheddar and bacon, from the hollow tree.
  10. B. Pizzarias — Keebler, 1991–2000. Pizza-flavored chips 'made with real pizza' — a Keebler 90s cult classic.
