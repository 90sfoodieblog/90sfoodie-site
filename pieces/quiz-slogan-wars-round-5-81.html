Slogan Wars, Round 5

This is Slogan Wars, Round 5 — a ten-question round built entirely from documented shelf history. Slogan Wars, Round 5 asks no trick questions and repeats no rumors: the archive only asks what it can prove. The Slogan Wars, Round 5 key is printed at the end, the archive way.

The Answer Key

  1. B. Mountain Dew — Pepsi, 1948. The extreme-sports era's fuel; the 90s made it a lifestyle.
  2. A. Crystal Pepsi — Pepsi, 1992–1994. The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
  3. B. Barq's Root Beer — Barq's, 1898. The caffeinated root beer with bite, from Biloxi in 1898.
  4. A. Lucky Charms — General Mills, 1964. The marshmallow count kept growing through the 90s — blue diamonds arrived in 1998.
  5. B. Haribo Goldbears — Haribo, 1922. The original gummi bears, from Bonn in 1922, in US stores by 1982.
  6. C. Animal Crackers (Barnum's) — Nabisco, 1902. The circus-cage box with a string handle, a Christmas ornament by design.
  7. A. Bagel Bites — Bagel Bites, 1985. When pizza's on a bagel, you can eat pizza any time — the jingle is the record.
  8. D. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  9. A. Fig Newtons — Nabisco, 1891. Named for Newton, Massachusetts — not the physicist.
  10. A. Klondike Bar — Klondike, 1922. The jingle's question ran all decade.
