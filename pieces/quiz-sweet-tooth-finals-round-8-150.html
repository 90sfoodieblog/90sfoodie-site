Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 8

The Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 8 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 8 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

The Answer Key

  1. A. Mary Jane — Mary Jane, 1914. Peanut butter and molasses chews in the yellow wrapper.
  2. C. Reese's Pieces — Hershey, 1978. E.T.'s candy in 1982 made it an icon; the 90s kept it there.
  3. A. SweeTarts — Nestlé, 1963. Tangy tablets from the Pixy Stix formula, pressed into coins.
  4. D. Twizzlers — Hershey, 1929. The twisted strawberry standard since 1929.
  5. B. Lemonhead — Ferrara, 1962. The lemon drop with the pucker face on the box.
  6. A. Necco Wafers — Necco, 1847–2018. The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
  7. B. Skor — Hershey, 1981. Thin butter toffee under milk chocolate, with a Swedish crown on the wrapper.
  8. C. Kit Kat — Hershey, 1935. The break-apart bar with the jingle everyone can finish.
  9. B. Crispy M&M's — Mars, 1999–2005. The orange-shelled crispy M&M, gone from the US in 2005 and revived a decade later.
  10. B. Necco Wafers — Necco, 1847–2018. The chalky wafer rolls of every glove box; the 2018 shutdown scare emptied shelves.
