The Answer Key

  1. B. Pizza Hut Buffet — Pizza Hut, early years. The red-roof lunch buffet with the salad bar and the sneeze-guard glamour.
  2. C. Frigo Cheese Heads — Frigo, 1990. String cheese you peeled strand by strand — never bite the stick whole.
  3. B. McDonald's Fried Apple Pie — McDonald's, 1968–1992. The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
  4. B. Sonic Drive-In — Sonic, 1953. Carhops on skates and a happy hour of half-price drinks.
  5. C. Fruit Roll-Ups — Betty Crocker, 1983. Peelable fruit leather with tongue tattoos by the late 90s.
  6. C. Shark Bites — Betty Crocker, 1988. Shark-shaped fruit snacks; the great white was the rare prize.
  7. B. Fruit by the Foot — Betty Crocker, 1991. Three feet of fruit snack, unrolled like a measuring tape.
  8. C. Wendy's SuperBar — Wendy's, 1980–1998. The all-you-can-eat salad, pasta, and taco bar inside Wendy's — a buffet time capsule.
  9. B. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  10. B. Chef Boyardee Beef Ravioli — Chef Boyardee, 1928. The real chef was Hector Boiardi of Cleveland; the can spelled it phonetically.
