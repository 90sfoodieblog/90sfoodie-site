The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 12

The The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 12 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 12 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Hi-C Juice Boxes — Hi-C, 1955. The juice box standard; Flashin' Fruit Punch stained a generation of shirts.
  2. C. Tostitos — Frito-Lay, 1979. Restaurant-style tortilla chips; Scoops! turned them into edible spoons in the 90s.
  3. B. Lay's — Frito-Lay, 1938. The bet was issued in 1963 and has never been lost.
  4. D. Sixlets — Sixlets, 1960. Candy-coated chocolate in a tube of eight-ball rows.
  5. C. Munchos — Frito-Lay, 1971. The crispy potato snack with the deep-voiced Muppet-ish pitchman.
  6. A. Pizza Hut Buffet — Pizza Hut, early years. The red-roof lunch buffet with the salad bar and the sneeze-guard glamour.
  7. C. Viennetta — Good Humor, 1982. The ripple-layered 'fancy' ice cream cake of a thousand dinner parties; America got it in the 90s.
  8. A. Quisp — Quaker, 1965–1975. The propeller-headed alien's cereal, later an internet revival darling.
  9. B. Little Debbie Swiss Rolls — Little Debbie, 1963. The budget pinwheel that filled more lunchboxes than any rival.
  10. A. Ding Dongs — Hostess, 1967. A chocolate hockey puck of cake and cream.
