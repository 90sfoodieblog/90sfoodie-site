The Answer Key

  1. C. Corn Pops — Kellogg's, 1950. Called Sugar Corn Pops until 1984's name-shortening era.
  2. B. Cinnamon Toast Crunch — General Mills, 1984. Cinnamon-sugar squares; Crazy Squares in the modern ads.
  3. B. Berry Berry Kix — General Mills, 1992. Kix mixed with purple berry-flavored clusters.
  4. B. Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries — Quaker, 1967. The berry pieces turned the milk pink-purple.
  5. C. Trix — General Mills, 1954. Turned fruit-shaped in 1991, the decade's defining cereal-box silhouette.
  6. B. Waffle Crisp — Post, 1996. Mini waffle squares with a maple-syrup taste.
  7. D. Morning Funnies — Ralston, 1988–1989. A cereal box printed with Sunday comics panels.
  8. C. Berry Berry Kix — General Mills, 1992. Kix mixed with purple berry-flavored clusters.
  9. D. Bill & Ted's Excellent Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1991. A most excellent tie-in, gone as fast as the phone booth.
  10. D. Honey Smacks — Kellogg's, 1953. Dig 'Em hopped onto the box in 1972; the cereal was Sugar Smacks before 1984.
