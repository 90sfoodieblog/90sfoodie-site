The Answer Key

  1. D. Choco Taco — Klondike, 1983–2022. The taco-shaped ice cream, mourned nationwide when Klondike retired it in 2022.
  2. A. Fruit Stripe — Fruit Stripe, 1960–2024. Tattooed stripes on every stick; the flavor was famously brief.
  3. D. Orbitz — Clearly Canadian, 1997–1998. The lava-lamp drink: flavored water with suspended gel balls floating in it.
  4. D. Oreo O's — Post, 1998–2007. Chocolate rings with marshmallow bits; left the US in 2007, returned a decade later.
  5. D. King Vitaman — Quaker, 1970–2019. The crowned king of the value shelf, poured for nearly fifty years.
  6. D. Fruitopia — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Kaleidoscope-colored fruit drinks with names like Strawberry Passion Awareness.
  7. D. Choco Taco — Klondike, 1983–2022. The taco-shaped ice cream, mourned nationwide when Klondike retired it in 2022.
  8. B. OK Soda — Coca-Cola, 1993–1995. Coke's Gen-X experiment with an underground-comics manifesto on every can.
  9. C. Keebler Magic Middles — Keebler, 1988–1995. Shortbread cookies with fudge middles — filed here by vending-machine logic.
  10. D. Wendy's SuperBar — Wendy's, 1980–1998. The all-you-can-eat salad, pasta, and taco bar inside Wendy's — a buffet time capsule.
