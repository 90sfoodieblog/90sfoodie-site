Midnight Snack Mix, Round 3

Midnight Snack Mix, Round 3 deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in Midnight Snack Mix, Round 3, the record does the talking.

  1. What was the famous line attached to Twizzlers?
    A. It ain't easy being cheesy
    B. Makes mouths happy
    C. You've never seen a taste like this
    D. The snack that smiles back
  2. In what year did Slim Jim first reach shelves?
    A. 1933
    B. 1929
    C. 1914
    D. 1926
  3. Name the product in this memory: “Elf-shaped sandwich cookies named for Everybody Loves Fudge.”
    A. Little Debbie Star Crunch
    B. E.L. Fudge
    C. SnackWell's Devil's Food Cookie Cakes
    D. Sno Balls
  4. Wendy's Frosty came from Wendy's. Which of these did too?
    A. Pepsi A.M.
    B. Wendy's SuperBar
    C. Sierra Mist
    D. Cap'n Crunch
  5. Crazy Bread came from Little Caesars. Which of these did too?
    A. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee
    B. Crispy M&M's
    C. Cocoa Puffs
    D. Little Caesars Pizza!Pizza!
  6. Name the product in this memory: “The blue box; the dinosaur arrived to stir the pot in the 90s.”
    A. Frigo Cheese Heads
    B. Kraft Macaroni & Cheese
    C. SpaghettiOs
    D. Snack Pack Pudding
  7. Which aisle of the 90s did Goldfish call home?
    A. Lunchbox
    B. Sodas & Drinks
    C. Snack Cakes & Cookies
    D. Snacks & Chips
  8. Which debuted first — Sprinkle Spangles, Mistic, Boo Berry, or Barq's Root Beer?
    A. Barq's Root Beer
    B. Mistic
    C. Sprinkle Spangles
    D. Boo Berry
  9. Which of these vanished from the shelves — Crazy Bread, Dum Dums, Ghostbusters Cereal, or Goldfish?
    A. Crazy Bread
    B. Ghostbusters Cereal
    C. Dum Dums
    D. Goldfish
  10. Which debuted first — Sour Patch Kids, Now and Later, Tampico, or Corn Nuts?
    A. Sour Patch Kids
    B. Now and Later
    C. Corn Nuts
    D. Tampico

The Answer Key

  1. B. Twizzlers — Hershey, 1929. The twisted strawberry standard since 1929.
  2. B. Slim Jim — GoodMark, 1929. The Macho Man years made 'snap into it' a catchphrase — oh yeah.
  3. B. E.L. Fudge — Keebler, 1986. Elf-shaped sandwich cookies named for Everybody Loves Fudge.
  4. B. Wendy's SuperBar — Wendy's, 1980–1998. The all-you-can-eat salad, pasta, and taco bar inside Wendy's — a buffet time capsule.
  5. D. Little Caesars Pizza!Pizza! — Little Caesars, 1979. Two pizzas for the price of one, in the long double box on paper.
  6. B. Kraft Macaroni & Cheese — Kraft, 1937. The blue box; the dinosaur arrived to stir the pot in the 90s.
  7. D. Goldfish — Pepperidge Farm, 1962. The smiling fish crackers got a mascot and a jingle in the 90s.
  8. A. Barq's Root Beer — Barq's, 1898. The caffeinated root beer with bite, from Biloxi in 1898.
  9. B. Ghostbusters Cereal — Ralston, 1985–1989. Ghost-shaped marshmallows and Slimer on the box.
  10. C. Corn Nuts — Corn Nuts, 1936. Toasted corn kernels, ranch and original, from the gas-station rack.
