The Answer Key

  1. D. Little Caesars Pizza!Pizza! — Little Caesars, 1979. Two pizzas for the price of one, in the long double box on paper.
  2. A. SoBe — SoBe, 1996. South Beach teas and elixirs with the dual-lizard logo.
  3. C. Little Caesars Pizza!Pizza! — Little Caesars, 1979. Two pizzas for the price of one, in the long double box on paper.
  4. D. Ding Dongs — Hostess, 1967. A chocolate hockey puck of cake and cream.
  5. D. Flintstones Push-Up — Nestlé, 1990. Sherbet push tubes with Bedrock's finest on the wrapper.
  6. A. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
  7. C. Rice Krispies Treats Cereal — Kellogg's, 1993. The marshmallow treat reimagined as a spoonable cereal.
  8. B. Butterfinger BB's — Nestlé, 1992–2006. Marble-sized Butterfingers, fronted by Bart Simpson at the height of Simpsons mania.
  9. B. KFC Chicken Littles — KFC, 1986–1990. Tiny chicken sandwiches for pocket change, gone too soon, revived decades later as Littles.
  10. A. SoBe — SoBe, 1996. South Beach teas and elixirs with the dual-lizard logo.
