The Answer Key

  1. B. Froot Loops — Kellogg's, 1963. Toucan Sam followed his nose; the loops were all one flavor despite the colors.
  2. C. Jack in the Box Antenna Balls — Jack in the Box, 1995. Jack's comeback era (from 1994) put his head on a million antennas.
  3. A. Oatmeal Creme Pies — Little Debbie, 1960. Little Debbie's original — two soft oatmeal cookies around creme.
  4. A. The Addams Family Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1992. Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
  5. C. Honey Nut Cheerios — General Mills, 1979. The honey-nut bowl that outsold the original.
  6. C. Bugles — General Mills, 1964. Corn horns worn on fingertips as claws in every classroom.
  7. D. Andy Capp's Hot Fries — Andy Capp's, 1971. Fry-shaped corn snacks with a comic-strip namesake.
  8. C. Fruity Yummy Mummy — General Mills, 1987–1992. The fifth monster cereal, gone before the decade turned.
  9. D. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
  10. C. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
