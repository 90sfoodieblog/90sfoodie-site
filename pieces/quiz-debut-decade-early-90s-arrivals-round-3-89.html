The Answer Key

  1. B. Cry Baby — Cry Baby, 1991. Extra-sour gumballs sold as a toughness test.
  2. A. Reese's Puffs — General Mills, 1994. Peanut butter and cocoa puffs — the candy-bar cereal of 1994.
  3. C. Ouch! Bubble Gum — Ouch!, 1990. Bandage-shaped gum in a tin that looked like a first-aid kit.
  4. C. Arizona Iced Tea — Arizona, 1992. The tall 99-cent can that never raised its price.
  5. D. Bigfoot Pizza — Pizza Hut, 1993–1995. Two square feet of pizza for $10.99, delivered in a giant box.
  6. A. Reese's Puffs — General Mills, 1994. Peanut butter and cocoa puffs — the candy-bar cereal of 1994.
  7. C. Fruitopia — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Kaleidoscope-colored fruit drinks with names like Strawberry Passion Awareness.
  8. A. Brisk Iced Tea — Lipton, 1991. Claymation celebrity ads made Brisk a 90s catchphrase machine.
  9. B. Warheads — Impact Confections, 1993. The sour-intensity standard, imported from Taiwan and a schoolyard dare in candy form.
  10. C. Frigo Cheese Heads — Frigo, 1990. String cheese you peeled strand by strand — never bite the stick whole.
