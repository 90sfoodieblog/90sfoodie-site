Debut Decade: Early 90s Arrivals, Round 5

The Answer Key

  1. D. Berry Berry Kix — General Mills, 1992. Kix mixed with purple berry-flavored clusters.
  2. A. Flamin' Hot Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1990. The spicy legend of the snack aisle — red fingers were a badge of honor.
  3. C. Sun Chips — Frito-Lay, 1991. Multigrain wavy chips; Harvest Cheddar defined the 'better chip' era.
  4. D. Rice Krispies Treats Cereal — Kellogg's, 1993. The marshmallow treat reimagined as a spoonable cereal.
  5. B. Hershey's Cookies 'n' Creme — Hershey, 1994. White creme and cookie bits — the bar that broke the brown-chocolate mold.
  6. C. OK Soda — Coca-Cola, 1993–1995. Coke's Gen-X experiment with an underground-comics manifesto on every can.
  7. A. E.L. Fudge — Keebler, 1986. Elf-shaped sandwich cookies named for Everybody Loves Fudge.
  8. B. Gushers — Betty Crocker, 1991. Fruit snacks with a liquid center that burst — heads turned into fruit in the ads. (Filed here by lunchbox logic.)
  9. B. Flintstones Push-Up — Nestlé, 1990. Sherbet push tubes with Bedrock's finest on the wrapper.
  10. A. Kid Cuisine — Kid Cuisine, 1990. The kids' TV dinner with the penguin, a brownie, and corn in the same tray universe.
