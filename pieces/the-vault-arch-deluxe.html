Arch Deluxe

McDonald's · 1996 – 2000

The short version: Arch Deluxe, from McDonald's, first sold in 1996, shelved with the fast food. The long version is the rest of this entry — and Arch Deluxe earns the long version, which is more than most of its shelf-mates can say.

Before the stories, the statistics of Arch Deluxe: produced by McDonald's, filed under fast food, first sold in 1996, and last sold in 2000. The archive front-loads the facts of Arch Deluxe for a practical reason — memory edits, but a card does not. When two fans disagree about Arch Deluxe at some future quiz night, this paragraph is the referee both of them agreed to in advance.

First Arch Deluxe was a discovery, then it was a preference, then it was a line item in the family grocery run that nobody questioned anymore. The burger for grown-ups, with a peppered bacon and a secret sauce — kids in ads were disgusted on purpose.

Ask the decade to picture the fast food shelf and it pictures Arch Deluxe first — that was the peak, and it lasted long enough that a whole cohort of kids assumed Arch Deluxe was a permanent feature of the world. Ronald McDonald fronted Arch Deluxe, and a 90s product with a face had a friend in every commercial break.

The last chapter of Arch Deluxe opened in 2000, when the orders stopped and the facings shrank to nothing. Fans of Arch Deluxe did what fans of discontinued things always do: they stocked up too late and remembered too well.

In the movie of an ordinary 90s childhood, Arch Deluxe is not in the highlight reel; it is in the background of every third scene, on counters and in cupboards, uncredited and indispensable. Background is not a small place — for Arch Deluxe, it was the whole kingdom.

The afterlife of Arch Deluxe is an argument that never quite ends, carried on in group chats and comment threads by people who agree on little else. That argument is the monument of Arch Deluxe, and it is load-bearing. If the 90s ever get a museum, Arch Deluxe will not need a plaque. Arch Deluxe will need a shelf, restocked daily, forever slightly picked over.

Consider Arch Deluxe officially kept: its maker named, its years fixed, its legend stated and sourced in the only ledger that bothered. Future archaeologists of the snack aisle will find Arch Deluxe here, spelled correctly.
