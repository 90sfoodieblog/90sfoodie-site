Trivia Night: The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 2

The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 2: fifteen questions, fifteen answers with the story underneath each one. Everything on the The Whole Store: Grand Mix, Round 2 card comes from the archive's record — the same facts the reference entries file in full.

QuestionAnswer & the story
Who was the mascot of Chuck E. Cheese?Chuck E. Cheese
Founded by Atari's Nolan Bushnell; absorbed ShowBiz Pizza in the 90s and kept the mouse.
What kind of product was Suzy Q's?Snack Cakes & Cookies
Banana and chocolate cake sandwiches with a cream middle.
What year did Red Vines debut?1952
West Coast red licorice, rival to the twist from the East.
True or false: Dairy Queen Blizzard is still sold today.True — it never left.
So thick it was served upside down — the flip was the proof.
What year did Spree debut?1971
The candy-coated cousin of SweeTarts.
Which company made Pizza Hut Buffet?Pizza Hut
The red-roof lunch buffet with the salad bar and the sneeze-guard glamour.
What kind of product was Ring Pop?Candy
The jewel you wore and ate, invented by a dad for his daughter's thumb habit.
What kind of product was Sixlets?Candy
Candy-coated chocolate in a tube of eight-ball rows.
What kind of product was King Cone?Frozen & Ice Cream
The ice cream truck's peanut-crowned cone.
KFC Chicken Littles came from KFC. Name another KFC product.Popcorn Chicken
Bite-size fried chicken in a bucket-shaped cup, a test item that never left.
Which came first, Skor or Corn Nuts?Corn Nuts — 1936, beating Skor (1981) by 45 year(s).
Toasted corn kernels, ranch and original, from the gas-station rack.
Sun Chips came from Frito-Lay. Name another Frito-Lay product.Ruffles
Ridges engineered for dips — 'Ruffles have ridges.'
Which came first, Jack in the Box Antenna Balls or Barq's Root Beer?Barq's Root Beer — 1898, beating Jack in the Box Antenna Balls (1995) by 97 year(s).
The caffeinated root beer with bite, from Biloxi in 1898.
Which came first, Uncrustables or Mistic?Mistic — 1990, beating Uncrustables (1995) by 5 year(s).
Tropical glass-bottle teas and juices from the Snapple era shelf.
Which came first, Otter Pops or Burger King Kids Club?Otter Pops — 1970, beating Burger King Kids Club (1990) by 20 year(s).
Freeze-it-yourself juice bars with pun-named characters.

Deep fact to close: Banana and chocolate cake sandwiches with a cream middle.
