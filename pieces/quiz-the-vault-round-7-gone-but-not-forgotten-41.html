The Vault, Round 7: Gone But Not Forgotten

The Vault, Round 7: Gone But Not Forgotten deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in The Vault, Round 7: Gone But Not Forgotten, the record does the talking.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Sprinkle Spangles — General Mills, 1993–1998. A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
  2. A. Frute Brute — General Mills, 1974–1982. The werewolf's fruit cereal, retired in the 80s and revived for Halloween seasons.
  3. C. The Addams Family Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1992. Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
  4. A. Batman Cereal — Ralston, 1989–1990. Bat-shaped cereal for Tim Burton's summer.
  5. B. Sprinkle Spangles — General Mills, 1993–1998. A star-shaped cereal coated in candy sprinkles, wished into being by a genie.
  6. B. Pepsi A.M. — Pepsi, 1989–1990. A higher-caffeine 'morning cola,' gone within a year.
  7. B. McLean Deluxe — McDonald's, 1991–1996. The 91%-fat-free burger bound with seaweed extract; the decade's diet-burger monument.
  8. B. Batman Cereal — Ralston, 1989–1990. Bat-shaped cereal for Tim Burton's summer.
  9. B. Super Size — McDonald's, 1987–2004. The upsell that defined an era, retired in 2004.
  10. B. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
