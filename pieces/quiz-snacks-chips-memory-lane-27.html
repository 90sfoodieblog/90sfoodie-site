Snacks & Chips: Memory Lane

This is Snacks & Chips: Memory Lane — a ten-question round built entirely from documented shelf history. Snacks & Chips: Memory Lane asks no trick questions and repeats no rumors: the archive only asks what it can prove. The Snacks & Chips: Memory Lane key is printed at the end, the archive way.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Cheez-It — Kellogg's, 1921. The square with the hole, baked since Dayton in 1921.
  2. C. Cheetos — Frito-Lay, 1948. Chester Cheetah took over the ads in 1986, too cool for the original mouse.
  3. B. Slim Jim — GoodMark, 1929. The Macho Man years made 'snap into it' a catchphrase — oh yeah.
  4. D. Bugles — General Mills, 1964. Corn horns worn on fingertips as claws in every classroom.
  5. A. Lay's — Frito-Lay, 1938. The bet was issued in 1963 and has never been lost.
  6. B. Cooler Ranch Doritos — Frito-Lay, 1986. Launched as Cooler Ranch in 1986; renamed Cool Ranch for international shelves.
  7. C. Handi-Snacks — Kraft, 1980. Crackers and a cheese spread with the little red plastic stick. (A lunchbox crossover king.)
  8. A. Ruffles — Frito-Lay, 1947. Ridges engineered for dips — 'Ruffles have ridges.'
  9. C. Keebler Magic Middles — Keebler, 1988–1995. Shortbread cookies with fudge middles — filed here by vending-machine logic.
  10. C. Doritos 3D's — Frito-Lay, 1998–2004. Puffed 3D Doritos in a plastic canister — the decade's chip engineering marvel.
