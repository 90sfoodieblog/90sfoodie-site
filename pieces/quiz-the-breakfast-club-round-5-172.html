The Answer Key

  1. C. Gushers — Betty Crocker, 1991. Fruit snacks with a liquid center that burst — heads turned into fruit in the ads. (Filed here by lunchbox logic.)
  2. C. Cosmic Brownies — Little Debbie, 1999. The rainbow-sprinkled brownie that closed the decade.
  3. B. Tastykake Butterscotch Krimpets — Tastykake, 1914. Philadelphia's sponge cakes with butterscotch icing, from 1914.
  4. A. Suzy Q's — Hostess, 1961. Banana and chocolate cake sandwiches with a cream middle.
  5. D. Fig Newtons — Nabisco, 1891. Named for Newton, Massachusetts — not the physicist.
  6. D. Animal Crackers (Barnum's) — Nabisco, 1902. The circus-cage box with a string handle, a Christmas ornament by design.
  7. B. Wheaties — General Mills, 1924. The orange box put Michael Jordan on more breakfast tables than anyone.
  8. D. Drake's Devil Dogs — Drake's, 1926. Two devil's food cakes with cream — the Northeast lunchbox dog.
  9. C. Frosted Flakes — Kellogg's, 1952. Tony's been roaring since 1952.
  10. B. Oreo — Nabisco, 1912. The twist-lick-dunk ritual; Double Stuf had been stacking since 1974.
