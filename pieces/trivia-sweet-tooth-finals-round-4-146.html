Trivia Night: Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 4

Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 4: fifteen questions, fifteen answers with the story underneath each one. Everything on the Sweet Tooth Finals, Round 4 card comes from the archive's record — the same facts the reference entries file in full.

QuestionAnswer & the story
What kind of product was Big League Chew?Gum
Shredded gum in a pouch, invented in a minor-league bullpen.
What kind of product was Dum Dums?Candy
The mystery flavor is one flavor's run crossing into the next.
What kind of product was Charms Blow Pop?Candy
A lollipop with gum at the center — two candies, one stick.
Which company made Nik-L-Nip?Nik-L-Nip
Wax bottles you bit, sipped, and chewed — the candy that was also a toy.

Eat some now, save some for later — taffy squares sold as a time-share.
Which company made Bazooka?Topps

Which company made Sixlets?Sixlets
Candy-coated chocolate in a tube of eight-ball rows.
Which company made Starburst?Mars

Which company made SweeTarts?Nestlé
Tangy tablets from the Pixy Stix formula, pressed into coins.
