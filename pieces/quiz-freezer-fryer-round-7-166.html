The Answer Key

  1. D. McDonald's Happy Meal — McDonald's, 1979. The boxed meal with a toy — the toy became the point by the 90s.
  2. B. Dove Bar — Dove, 1984. Thick chocolate over vanilla — the premium bar of the freezer case.
  3. C. Boston Market — Boston Market, 1985. Boston Chicken's rotisserie boom renamed the chain Boston Market in 1995.
  4. B. Hardee's Frisco Burger — Hardee's, 1992. The sourdough melt that headlined Hardee's early-90s premium run.
  5. B. Eskimo Pie — Eskimo Pie, 1921. The first chocolate-covered ice cream bar with a foil wrapper.
  6. A. Chicken McNuggets — McDonald's, 1983. Debuted nationally in 1983; the sauces were half the ritual.
  7. B. McDonald's Fried Apple Pie — McDonald's, 1968–1992. The original pie was fried until 1992, when the baked version took over in the US.
  8. B. The Whopper — Burger King, 1957. The flame-grilled standard since 1957.
  9. D. Big Mac — McDonald's, 1968. The jingle lists the whole build; a Pittsburgh creation that went national in 1968.
  10. A. Chuck E. Cheese — Chuck E. Cheese, 1977. Founded by Atari's Nolan Bushnell; absorbed ShowBiz Pizza in the 90s and kept the mouse.
