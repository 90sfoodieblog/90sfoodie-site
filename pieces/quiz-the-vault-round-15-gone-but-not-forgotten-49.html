The Vault, Round 15: Gone But Not Forgotten

Ten questions on The Vault, Round 15: Gone But Not Forgotten — every one drawn from the archive's own record, every answer checked against the shelf it came from. The The Vault, Round 15: Gone But Not Forgotten key waits at the bottom; no peeking, that's the rule of the rewind.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Pop-Tarts Crunch — Kellogg's, 1994–1995. The toaster pastry shrunk into a cereal, frosted strawberry and brown sugar cinnamon.
  2. A. Orbitz — Clearly Canadian, 1997–1998. The lava-lamp drink: flavored water with suspended gel balls floating in it.
  3. B. Fruit Stripe — Fruit Stripe, 1960–2024. Tattooed stripes on every stick; the flavor was famously brief.
  4. A. Taco Bell Chihuahua — Taco Bell, 1997–2000. Gidget the chihuahua was 1997's most quoted dog; she also wanted Godzilla tickets.
  5. D. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  6. D. The Noid — Domino's, 1986–1995. The claymation villain who ruined pizzas — Domino's mascot of chaos.
  7. B. Surge — Coca-Cola, 1996–2003. Coke's citrus answer to Mountain Dew; fans campaigned it back in 2014.
  8. C. Hidden Treasures — General Mills, 1993–1995. Plain-looking squares, some hiding cherry, orange, or grape fillings inside.
  9. C. Planters Cheez Balls — Planters, 1978–2006. The blue canister of cheese balls, gone in 2006 and revived in 2018 by popular demand.
  10. B. Fruit Stripe — Fruit Stripe, 1960–2024. Tattooed stripes on every stick; the flavor was famously brief.
