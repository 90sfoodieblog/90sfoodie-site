The Drink Tank, Round 5

The The Drink Tank, Round 5 round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In The Drink Tank, Round 5 the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

The Answer Key

  1. C. Little Hug — Little Hug, 1974. The little barrel drinks sold for pocket change.
  2. D. Mountain Dew — Pepsi, 1948. The extreme-sports era's fuel; the 90s made it a lifestyle.
  3. B. Bug Juice — Bug Juice, 1990. The summer-camp canteen staple in the bug-logo bottle.
  4. C. Gatorade — Pepsico, 1965. The 1991 'Be Like Mike' jingle made sports drink a Michael Jordan anthem.
  5. B. Pepsi Wild Cherry — Pepsi, 1988. Tested as Wild Cherry Pepsi from 1988; the cherry cola wars' survivor.
  6. B. Hawaiian Punch — Hawaiian Punch, 1934. Punchy offered a punch, and took one, in every spot.
  7. B. Brisk Iced Tea — Lipton, 1991. Claymation celebrity ads made Brisk a 90s catchphrase machine.
  8. D. Crystal Pepsi — Pepsi, 1992–1994. The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
  9. C. Josta — Pepsi, 1995–1999. Pepsi's guarana soda — the first energy drink from a major soda company.
  10. C. Mistic — Mistic, 1990. Tropical glass-bottle teas and juices from the Snapple era shelf.
