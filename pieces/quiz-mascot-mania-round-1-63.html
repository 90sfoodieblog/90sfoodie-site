Mascot Mania, Round 1

This is Mascot Mania, Round 1 — a ten-question round built entirely from documented shelf history. Mascot Mania, Round 1 asks no trick questions and repeats no rumors: the archive only asks what it can prove. The Mascot Mania, Round 1 key is printed at the end, the archive way.

The Answer Key

  1. A. Cookie Crisp — Ralston, 1977. The cereal that promised cookies for breakfast, sold by a wizard, then a burglar.
  2. B. Trix — General Mills, 1954. Turned fruit-shaped in 1991, the decade's defining cereal-box silhouette.
  3. A. Chocodiles — Hostess, early years. The chocolate-covered Twinkie, hunted for decades by its mascot Chauncey.
  4. D. Little Debbie Swiss Rolls — Little Debbie, 1963. The budget pinwheel that filled more lunchboxes than any rival.
  5. B. Wendy's Frosty — Wendy's, 1969. Too thick for a straw, too thin for a spoon — fries dipped in it are the law.
  6. D. Nutty Bars — Little Debbie, 1964. Peanut butter wafers in chocolate, sold as a two-stick team.
  7. B. Trix — General Mills, 1954. Turned fruit-shaped in 1991, the decade's defining cereal-box silhouette.
  8. A. Crispy M&M's — Mars, 1999–2005. The orange-shelled crispy M&M, gone from the US in 2005 and revived a decade later.
  9. C. Little Debbie Star Crunch — Little Debbie, early years. The cosmic rice-crisp and caramel disc that pre-dated Cosmic Brownies.
  10. B. Nintendo Cereal System — Ralston, 1988–1989. Two cereals in one box — Super Mario fruit and Zelda berry, split down the middle.
