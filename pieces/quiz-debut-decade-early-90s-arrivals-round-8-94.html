The Answer Key

  1. B. Arizona Iced Tea — Arizona, 1992. The tall 99-cent can that never raised its price.
  2. B. Reese's Puffs — General Mills, 1994. Peanut butter and cocoa puffs — the candy-bar cereal of 1994.
  3. D. Squeezit — Betty Crocker, 1985–2001. Twist-off character bottles you squeezed to drink — Chucklin' Cherry led the cast.
  4. A. Fruitopia Fruit Integration — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Fruitopia's flagship flavor name, peak 90s wordplay in a bottle.
  5. C. The Addams Family Cereal — Ralston, 1991–1992. Skull-and-crossbones-shaped pieces for the 1991 movie.
  6. B. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  7. A. Ouch! Bubble Gum — Ouch!, 1990. Bandage-shaped gum in a tin that looked like a first-aid kit.
  8. A. Bubble Jug — Trolli, 1990. Powder that turned to gum in your mouth, shaken from a jug.
  9. B. Pizzarias — Keebler, 1991–2000. Pizza-flavored chips 'made with real pizza' — a Keebler 90s cult classic.
  10. D. Frigo Cheese Heads — Frigo, 1990. String cheese you peeled strand by strand — never bite the stick whole.
