House of Mars

House of Mars deals in the verifiable: who made it, when it lived, what it promised on the box. Ten questions, one printed answer key, zero guesses — in House of Mars, the record does the talking.

    A. Starburst
    B. Lemonhead
    C. SweeTarts
    D. Good & Plenty

The Answer Key

  1. A. Starburst — Mars, 1967. Fruit chews that reached America in 1967 as Starburst after a UK life as Opal Fruits.
  2. D. Twix — Mars, 1979. Two bars per pack — the sharing argument built in.
  3. D. Starburst — Mars, 1967. Fruit chews that reached America in 1967 as Starburst after a UK life as Opal Fruits.
  4. B. Combos — Mars, 1978. Cheese-filled pretzel and cracker tubes — road-trip fuel.
  5. A. Peanut Butter M&M's — Mars, 1989. Peanut butter joined the shell game at the decade's edge.
  6. B. Snickers — Mars, 1930. Named for the Mars family's horse; the 90s bar of choice at every checkout.
  7. C. Snickers — Mars, 1930. Named for the Mars family's horse; the 90s bar of choice at every checkout.
  8. B. Crispy M&M's — Mars, 1999–2005. The orange-shelled crispy M&M, gone from the US in 2005 and revived a decade later.
  9. A. Snickers — Mars, 1930. Named for the Mars family's horse; the 90s bar of choice at every checkout.
  10. A. Crispy M&M's — Mars, 1999–2005. The orange-shelled crispy M&M, gone from the US in 2005 and revived a decade later.
