Candy: Memory Lane

Candy: Memory Lane: ten questions from the record the archive keeps. Makers, years, mascots, slogans — if Candy: Memory Lane asks it, the file answered it first. In Candy: Memory Lane, answers wait at the bottom and the honor system is in force.

The Answer Key

  1. D. Pixy Stix — Nestlé, 1952. Powdered sugar in a paper straw — pure sugar, poured.
  2. D. Whatchamacallit — Hershey, 1978. The bar named for the word you use when you can't remember the word.
  3. C. Butterfinger BB's — Nestlé, 1992–2006. Marble-sized Butterfingers, fronted by Bart Simpson at the height of Simpsons mania.
  4. D. Atomic Fireballs — Ferrara, 1954. The cinnamon jawbreaker that separated the brave from the wise.
  5. A. Skittles — Mars, 1982. The rainbow's US arrival; Wild Berry joined in 1989.
  6. B. Dots — Tootsie, 1945. Gumdrops in the yellow box, straight from the theater counter.
  7. D. Sixlets — Sixlets, 1960. Candy-coated chocolate in a tube of eight-ball rows.
  8. B. Dots — Tootsie, 1945. Gumdrops in the yellow box, straight from the theater counter.
  9. A. Snowcaps — Nestlé, 1948. Dark chocolate drops with white sprinkles — pure movie candy.
  10. A. Butterfinger BB's — Nestlé, 1992–2006. Marble-sized Butterfingers, fronted by Bart Simpson at the height of Simpsons mania.
