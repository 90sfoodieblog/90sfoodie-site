This is Cafeteria Kings: Lunchbox & Fast Food, Round 7 — a ten-question round built entirely from documented shelf history. Cafeteria Kings: Lunchbox & Fast Food, Round 7 asks no trick questions and repeats no rumors: the archive only asks what it can prove. The Answer Key

  1. A. Book It! — Pizza Hut, 1984. Pizza Hut's reading program: read books, earn a personal pan pizza — the 90s' tastiest homework.
  2. C. McPizza — McDonald's, 1989–2000. McDonald's pizza slowed the drive-thru to a crawl; gone by 2000 in most stores.
  3. A. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  4. A. Sonic Drive-In — Sonic, 1953. Carhops on skates and a happy hour of half-price drinks.
  5. B. Chicken McNuggets — McDonald's, 1983. Debuted nationally in 1983; the sauces were half the ritual.
  6. C. Jell-O Pudding Snacks — Jell-O, 1980. Pudding pops' cup cousins in the refrigerated case.
  7. A. The Whopper — Burger King, 1957. The flame-grilled standard since 1957.
  8. C. Book It! — Pizza Hut, 1984. Pizza Hut's reading program: read books, earn a personal pan pizza — the 90s' tastiest homework.
  9. D. The Noid — Domino's, 1986–1995. The claymation villain who ruined pizzas — Domino's mascot of chaos.
  10. A. Kraft Macaroni & Cheese — Kraft, 1937. The blue box; the dinosaur arrived to stir the pot in the 90s.
