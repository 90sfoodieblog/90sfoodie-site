The Answer Key

  1. B. Oreo — Nabisco, 1912. The twist-lick-dunk ritual; Double Stuf had been stacking since 1974.
  2. A. OK Soda — Coca-Cola, 1993–1995. Coke's Gen-X experiment with an underground-comics manifesto on every can.
  3. C. Juicy Juice — Juicy Juice, 1977. The juice box of the nursery-school set.
  4. B. Jolt Cola — Jolt, 1985–2009. The original energy cola, sold on a single boast.
  5. D. Push Pop — Topps, 1986. Save some for later — the cap made it a promise kids broke daily.
  6. C. Little Caesars Pizza!Pizza! — Little Caesars, 1979. Two pizzas for the price of one, in the long double box on paper.
  7. A. Push Pop — Topps, 1986. Save some for later — the cap made it a promise kids broke daily.
  8. C. Kit Kat — Hershey, 1935. The break-apart bar with the jingle everyone can finish.
  9. D. Skittles — Mars, 1982. The rainbow's US arrival; Wild Berry joined in 1989.
  10. C. Fig Newtons — Nabisco, 1891. Named for Newton, Massachusetts — not the physicist.
