The Answer Key

  1. C. Toaster Strudel — Pillsbury, 1985. Icing packets you applied yourself — the toaster pastry arms race of 1985.
  2. B. Ecto Cooler — Hi-C, 1987–2001. The green tangerine juice box with Slimer on the front, outliving the cartoon by a decade.
  3. C. Trix — General Mills, 1954. Turned fruit-shaped in 1991, the decade's defining cereal-box silhouette.
  4. A. Andy Capp's Hot Fries — Andy Capp's, 1971. Fry-shaped corn snacks with a comic-strip namesake.
  5. D. Kool-Aid — Kraft, 1927. The pitcher who burst through walls; Bursts bottles joined the pouches in the 90s.
  6. B. Tastykake Butterscotch Krimpets — Tastykake, 1914. Philadelphia's sponge cakes with butterscotch icing, from 1914.
  7. A. Ding Dongs — Hostess, 1967. A chocolate hockey puck of cake and cream.
  8. B. Flintstones Push-Up — Nestlé, 1990. Sherbet push tubes with Bedrock's finest on the wrapper.
  9. A. Dunkaroos — Betty Crocker, 1990–2012. Cookies and frosting dip — the lunchbox legend, revived in 2020 after an 8-year absence.
  10. D. Golden Crisp — Post, 1949. Sugar Bear crooned it, cool as a jazz singer.
