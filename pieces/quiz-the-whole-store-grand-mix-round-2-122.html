The Answer Key

  1. C. Ben & Jerry's — Ben & Jerry's, 1978. Vermont's finest; Cherry Garcia (1987) and Phish Food (1997) were the decade's pints.
  2. A. McRib — McDonald's, 1981. The rib-shaped pork sandwich whose farewell tours never end.
  3. D. Hawaiian Punch — Hawaiian Punch, 1934. Punchy offered a punch, and took one, in every spot.
  4. B. Milk Duds — Hershey, 1926. Caramel 'duds' — named for the round shape the machine couldn't make.
  5. D. Tootsie Roll — Tootsie, 1896. The owl's question outlived the century.
  6. D. Otter Pops — Otter Pops, 1970. Freeze-it-yourself juice bars with pun-named characters.
  7. D. King Cone — Good Humor, early years. The ice cream truck's peanut-crowned cone.
  8. C. Crazy Bread — Little Caesars, 1982. Butter-and-parmesan breadsticks with Crazy Sauce.
  9. D. Now and Later — Now and Later, 1962. Eat some now, save some for later — taffy squares sold as a time-share.
  10. D. Big Mac — McDonald's, 1968. The jingle lists the whole build; a Pittsburgh creation that went national in 1968.
