The Answer Key

  1. A. Chicken McNuggets — McDonald's, 1983. Debuted nationally in 1983; the sauces were half the ritual.
  2. B. McPizza — McDonald's, 1989–2000. McDonald's pizza slowed the drive-thru to a crawl; gone by 2000 in most stores.
  3. B. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  4. D. McDLT — McDonald's, 1984–1990. Two-chamber styrofoam packaging — Jason Alexander sang its praises in 1985.
  5. B. Hardee's Frisco Burger — Hardee's, 1992. The sourdough melt that headlined Hardee's early-90s premium run.
  6. B. Stuffed Crust Pizza — Pizza Hut, 1995. Cheese sealed in the crust — eat the pizza backwards, said the ads.
  7. A. Pizza Hut Buffet — Pizza Hut, early years. The red-roof lunch buffet with the salad bar and the sneeze-guard glamour.
  8. A. The Whopper — Burger King, 1957. The flame-grilled standard since 1957.
  9. C. Domino's 30-Minute Guarantee — Domino's, 1973–1993. Free or discounted if late, until safety concerns ended it in 1993.
  10. C. Wendy's Frosty — Wendy's, 1969. Too thick for a straw, too thin for a spoon — fries dipped in it are the law.
