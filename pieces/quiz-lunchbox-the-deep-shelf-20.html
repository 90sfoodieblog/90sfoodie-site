The Answer Key

  1. B. Lunchables — Oscar Mayer, 1988. Build-your-own turkey, cheese, and crackers — the lunchbox power symbol from 1988.
  2. B. Chef Boyardee Beef Ravioli — Chef Boyardee, 1928. The real chef was Hector Boiardi of Cleveland; the can spelled it phonetically.
  3. D. Kraft Macaroni & Cheese — Kraft, 1937. The blue box; the dinosaur arrived to stir the pot in the 90s.
  4. C. Snack Pack Pudding — Hunt's, 1968. The pudding cup of Billy Madison fame; the tab-top lid was a hazard and a rite.
  5. D. Handi-Snacks — Kraft, 1980. Crackers and a cheese spread with the little red plastic stick. (A lunchbox crossover king.)
  6. C. Shark Bites — Betty Crocker, 1988. Shark-shaped fruit snacks; the great white was the rare prize.
  7. D. Kraft Macaroni & Cheese — Kraft, 1937. The blue box; the dinosaur arrived to stir the pot in the 90s.
  8. D. Scooby-Doo Fruit Snacks — Betty Crocker, 1999. The opaque blue Scooby gummy was a flavor mystery of its own.
  9. C. Fruit Roll-Ups — Betty Crocker, 1983. Peelable fruit leather with tongue tattoos by the late 90s.
  10. C. Snack Pack Pudding — Hunt's, 1968. The pudding cup of Billy Madison fame; the tab-top lid was a hazard and a rite.
