The Answer Key

  1. D. Kix — General Mills, 1937. The first puffed corn cereal, puffed in a gun-like machine.
  2. A. Waffle Crisp — Post, 1996. Mini waffle squares with a maple-syrup taste.
  3. B. Cocoa Puffs — General Mills, 1958. Sonny went cuckoo in every 90s commercial break.
  4. B. Fruity Yummy Mummy — General Mills, 1987–1992. The fifth monster cereal, gone before the decade turned.
  5. D. Cap'n Crunch's Crunch Berries — Quaker, 1967. The berry pieces turned the milk pink-purple.
  6. D. Pop-Tarts Crunch — Kellogg's, 1994–1995. The toaster pastry shrunk into a cereal, frosted strawberry and brown sugar cinnamon.
  7. D. Morning Funnies — Ralston, 1988–1989. A cereal box printed with Sunday comics panels.
  8. C. French Toast Crunch — General Mills, 1995–2006. Tiny toast-shaped pieces in maple syrup flavor; fans won a revival in 2014.
  9. C. Golden Crisp — Post, 1949. Sugar Bear crooned it, cool as a jazz singer.
  10. B. Trix — General Mills, 1954. Turned fruit-shaped in 1991, the decade's defining cereal-box silhouette.
