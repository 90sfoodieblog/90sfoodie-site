Trivia Night: Sodas & Drinks: The Deep Shelf

The Sodas & Drinks: The Deep Shelf night runs fifteen deep: rapid-fire questions from the shelf's own history, answers underneath with the receipts attached. Warm up the reading voice for Sodas & Drinks: The Deep Shelf — these are meant to be said out loud.

QuestionAnswer & the story
What year did New Coke debut?1985
The 79-day reformulation fiasco that made Coca-Cola Classic a comeback story.
True or false: Bug Juice is still sold today.True — it never left.
The summer-camp canteen staple in the bug-logo bottle.
What slogan sold SunnyD?“The tangy original”
The orange drink kids pulled from the fridge while parents reached for 'the purple stuff' (in the famous ad, the sunny one won).
What year did Tampico debut?1989
Gallon citrus punch — the cookout and corner-store staple from 1989.
When was Josta discontinued?1999
Pepsi's guarana soda — the first energy drink from a major soda company.
Which company made Faygo?Faygo
Detroit's soda — Rock & Rye — and the Insane Clown Posse's eternal spray.
Which company made Juicy Juice?Juicy Juice
The juice box of the nursery-school set.
True or false: Arizona Iced Tea is still sold today.True — it never left.
The tall 99-cent can that never raised its price.
What year did Pepsi A.M. debut?1989
A higher-caffeine 'morning cola,' gone within a year.
Pepsi Wild Cherry came from Pepsi. Name another Pepsi product.Crystal Pepsi
The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
Which came first, Yoo-hoo or Mistic?Yoo-hoo — 1928, beating Mistic (1990) by 62 year(s).
The chocolate drink (not milk) of ballfields and bodegas.
Which came first, Kool-Aid Bursts or Hi-C Juice Boxes?Hi-C Juice Boxes — 1955, beating Kool-Aid Bursts (1994) by 39 year(s).
The juice box standard; Flashin' Fruit Punch stained a generation of shirts.
Which came first, Orbitz or Pepsi Wild Cherry?Pepsi Wild Cherry — 1988, beating Orbitz (1997) by 9 year(s).
Tested as Wild Cherry Pepsi from 1988; the cherry cola wars' survivor.
Pepsi Wild Cherry came from Pepsi. Which other product did Pepsi give the decade?Mountain Dew
The extreme-sports era's fuel; the 90s made it a lifestyle.
Fruitopia came from Coca-Cola. Name another Coca-Cola product.Mr. Pibb
The Dr Pepper rival renamed Pibb Xtra in 2001.
