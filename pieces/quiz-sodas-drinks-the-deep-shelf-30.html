Sodas & Drinks: The Deep Shelf

The Sodas & Drinks: The Deep Shelf round: ten questions from the archive's files, each with its answer waiting in the key below. In Sodas & Drinks: The Deep Shelf the facts were old before they were trivia — that is what makes them fair game.

The Answer Key

  1. D. Mistic — Mistic, 1990. Tropical glass-bottle teas and juices from the Snapple era shelf.
  2. C. Capri Sun — Capri Sun, 1981. The silver pouch and the straw-stab ritual of every lunchbox.
  3. B. Handi-Snacks — Kraft, 1980. Crackers and a cheese spread with the little red plastic stick. (A lunchbox crossover king.)
  4. D. Mr. Pibb — Coca-Cola, 1972–2001. The Dr Pepper rival renamed Pibb Xtra in 2001.
  5. B. Crystal Pepsi — Pepsi, 1992–1994. The clear cola craze's flagship; Van Halen's 'Right Now' launched it at the Super Bowl.
  6. D. Hi-C Juice Boxes — Hi-C, 1955. The juice box standard; Flashin' Fruit Punch stained a generation of shirts.
  7. B. Arizona Iced Tea — Arizona, 1992. The tall 99-cent can that never raised its price.
  8. C. Ecto Cooler — Hi-C, 1987–2001. The green tangerine juice box with Slimer on the front, outliving the cartoon by a decade.
  9. A. Mr. Pibb — Coca-Cola, 1972–2001. The Dr Pepper rival renamed Pibb Xtra in 2001.
  10. D. Fruitopia Fruit Integration — Coca-Cola, 1994–2003. Fruitopia's flagship flavor name, peak 90s wordplay in a bottle.
