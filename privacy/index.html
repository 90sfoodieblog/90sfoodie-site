Privacy Policy

Plain language, no fine-print games. This page explains what 90sfoodie.com collects, why, and the choices you have.

Effective October 14, 2026. 90s Foodie (90sfoodie.com) is an enthusiast archive about 1990s snacks, drinks, and fast food, run by Denny. Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact Us form on the homepage.

What this site collects

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people visit and how the archive is used. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location (from IP address), device, and browser. We use it only to improve the site. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Advertising cookies. We may use Google AdSense to serve ads on this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info.

Affiliate links. Some pages link to shops (such as Amazon, Walmart, and eBay) where a snack can still be bought. Those are affiliate links: if you buy through them, the archive may earn a small commission from the seller at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships never influence what the archive says about a snack. Link monetization on this site is provided through Skimlinks.

Contact form. If you write to us through the Contact Us form, we receive the name, email address, topic, and message you submit. Submissions are delivered through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service, straight to our inbox. We use your details only to read and reply to your message. They are never sold, rented, or added to a mailing list.

Your ratings stay on your device. The "Remember it?" star ratings are stored only in your own browser's local storage. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Hosting logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect visitor IP addresses and standard server logs for security and operation of its service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What this site does not do

There are no accounts, no purchases, and no checkout on this site. We never sell personal information. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and this site is not directed at them.

Cookies and your choices

Most browsers let you view, block, or delete cookies in their settings. You can use this site with cookies disabled; the archive works the same — only the star ratings and analytics are affected.

External links

This site links to other services — Substack (where the blog lives), Reddit, Bluesky, Tumblr, Instagram, and Threads. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
